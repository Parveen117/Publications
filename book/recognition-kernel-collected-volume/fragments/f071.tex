\hypertarget{transfer-rule}{%
\section{Transfer rule}\label{transfer-rule}}

Each RKF capsule retains only:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  typed definitions;
\item
  theorem statements;
\item
  finite or abstract proofs actually supported by the source;
\item
  explicit hypotheses;
\item
  exact claim boundaries.
\end{enumerate}

Numerical fits, speculative physical extensions, and RH conclusions are not promoted by this transfer.
