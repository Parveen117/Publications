Selected in this packet: V=0, no matter, real periodic q,phi with no winding,
period one and hbar=1. Two explicitly different vacua are compared. First,
both null currents use positive-index annihilators, giving indefinite norms
and c=2. Second, diagonal currents u=a+b and v=a-b use u_n and v_-n
as annihilators for n>0, giving positive kinematic norms and c=0. Each is
normal ordered in its own vacuum with S_0=a_0 b_0 on the oscillator vacuum.
This is not a unitary basis change inside the first representation. Shared
zero modes are real multiplication variables on Schwartz functions. These
choices are not claimed to be fixed uniquely by native cut grammar.
