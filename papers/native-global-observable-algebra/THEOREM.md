## 1. Complete algebra of smooth classical observable values

Every smooth constraint orbit has a homogeneous representative by LPS,
CCS and COS. Write its coordinates (Q,P,Phi,K), with PK=0. For P!=0 the
gauge direction changes Phi; for K!=0 it changes Q; at P=K=0 the critical
orbits retain both labels. COS-T2 proves that globally continuous invariant
functions have one common value at all critical orbits.

Consider real or complex smooth functionals on the full local phase space
whose values on the smooth constraint surface are invariant under the
connected local gauge flows. Identify functionals with the same values
there. Smoothness includes ordinary C^infinity dependence when restricted
to the four-dimensional homogeneous family. For Poisson brackets and flows
we require smooth Hamiltonian gradients so the source bracket is defined.
We classify this **value algebra**, not the ideal generated by the infinitely
many local constraints.

**GOA-T1 (smooth Dirac value normal form).** Every class is specified
uniquely by a constant c and two smooth functions a(Q,P), b(Phi,K), through

    F|_(K=0)=c+P a(Q,P),
    F|_(P=0)=c+K b(Phi,K).                                  (1)

Conversely every such triple has an actual smooth full-local weak Dirac
representative

    F_local=c+P a(Q_sharp,P)+K b(Phi_av,K),                     (2)

where Phi_av is COS-T6's mean scalar. For a smooth off-shell Hamiltonian
extension use the periodic primitive

    r_tilde'=k-K, integral r_tilde=0,
    Q_sharp=(1/2)log[(integral exp(q+r_tilde))
                    (integral exp(q-r_tilde))].

The periodic inverse derivative on mean-zero functions is smooth; its adjoint
also maps smooth periodic inputs to smooth periodic functions. This representative
has smooth Hamiltonian gradients. On K=0 its primitive differs from COS's
by a constant, so Q_sharp equals Q_* there and gives the same physical
values in (2). This convenient off-shell extension changes no predecessor
observable value. Uniform k shifts leave r_tilde unchanged. Consequently

    {Q_sharp,P}=1, {Phi_av,K}=1,
    {Q_sharp,Phi_av}={Q_sharp,K}={P,Phi_av}={P,K}=0

hold exactly for these full-local functionals. These identities also follow
directly from constant q shifts and the mean-zero inverse derivative.
The representation in (2) is unique only in physical values; its off-shell extension is not
claimed unique. The products and Poisson brackets close in this class.

Proof. Restrict an observable to homogeneous tuples. On K=0 it is
independent of Phi for P!=0 and therefore for all P by smoothness. Denote
that smooth function by A(Q,P). Similarly the other restriction is a
smooth B(Phi,K). COS-T2 gives A(Q,0)=B(Phi,0)=c. Hadamard's formula gives

    A(Q,P)-c=P integral_0^1 partial_P A(Q,tP) dt,

representatives is exhaustiveness of physical values because every smooth
local orbit reaches such a representative.

Conversely agreement with COS on K=0 identifies Q_sharp with the invariant
LPS Q on P!=0, while
Phi_av=Phi on K!=0. P and K are invariant under every local generator.
The first term P a(Q_sharp,P) is invariant on P!=0 and has zero gauge variation
on P=0 because P and its variation vanish there. The other term works
identically. This proves weak invariance on the full source constraint

the auxiliary constant, whose change would cancel from Q_sharp anyway.

For two triples (c,a,b),(d,u,v), multiplication has constant cd and

    {P a,P u}=P[a_Q u-a u_Q+P(a_Q u_P-a_P u_Q)],             (3)

and similarly on the second. The constant bracket is zero. Cross terms

For other smooth Hamiltonian-admissible representatives, the regular

This is a classification of globally smooth invariant values. It does
not include every observable defined on just one open branch, nor supply

## 2. Classical flows preserve branch and orientation

**GOA-T2 (smooth classical flow boundary).** Along any finite segment of
a smooth Hamiltonian flow generated by (1), an initially nonzero P on
the first branch keeps its sign; likewise K on the other branch. These
flows cannot transfer a regular orbit to the complementary branch or the
critical stratum while remaining a regular finite smooth flow.

On the P branch Hamilton's equations are

    dot Q=a+P a_P, dot P=-P a_Q,
    P(t)=P(0)exp[-integral_0^t a_Q(Q(s),P(s)) ds].            (4)

The coefficient is continuous along a finite smooth trajectory. Thus the
exponential never vanishes or changes sign. The K proof is identical.
The critical stratum is preserved, but its individual orbit labels need
not be fixed: the homogeneous extension c+Pa+Kb generates
dot Q=a(Q,0), dot Phi=b(Phi,0) there. All globally smooth observable
values nevertheless remain their critical constants. These are observable
Hamiltonian flows, not the original constraint gauge flows.

This is a classical statement. It does not by itself imply that a chosen
quantization of every smooth observable commutes with the quantum sign
projectors. Sections 4 and 5 supply an explicit counterexample to that
stronger inference in the inherited full-line quantum representation.
