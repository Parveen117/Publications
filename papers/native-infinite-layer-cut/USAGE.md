# ILC-1 replay and source contract

```bash
python3.12 papers/native-infinite-layer-cut/run.py --check
python3.12 scripts/replay_quantum_geometry.py
```

Use Python 3.12. It verifies source hashes and invokes the native
JavaScript replay. `--check` reproduces the exact certificate without
rewriting it. `--write` intentionally regenerates the leaf's certificate
after its source manifest has been reviewed. Predecessor packets are
frozen and are never regenerated by this stage.

`source_layers.cjs` evaluates the complete source, with PIW's constant
and low-level arithmetic methods. The new higher Euler coefficients
sum all powers of each retained prime by exact integer polynomials.
The omitted primes have explicit signed logarithmic-integral tails.
Higher Gamma coefficients use a positive Hurwitz series with a full
Euler--Maclaurin remainder. Exact truncated composition changes to
the local lambda coordinate. The execution uses s=29 and degree 15.

`layer_adapter.cjs` constructs the two forms, their outward LDL factors,
binomial readings and finite response. Response acceptance requires
both positive forms; missing layers are never filled with zero. The
exact cut fixture also checks the carried metric, positive contraction,
positive square root and its commutation relation. An uncarried metric
is rejected. Irrational actual-source square roots are justified by the
written functional-calculus theorem, not presented as rational matrices.

The exact four-mode fixtures are labelled toys. The actual certificate
contains all 16 fresh layers, all analytic tails, the three 8x8 form
factorizations, finite spectral bounds and the 136 finite binomial
readings. PIW's archived s=29 and independently evaluated s=31 jets supply cross-checks;
the unfitted point is not used to generate the local layers.

All-order positivity and the equivalent RH statement remain open.
This calculation does not extend the nine-prime bank, enlarge the
old V24, lower global eta or identify electromagnetic propagation.
