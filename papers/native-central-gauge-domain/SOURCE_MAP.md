Journal of Functional Analysis 187 (2001), 406-441,

https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022123601938078

The published abstract states the compact-manifold result used here:
a unitary representation of the identity component of the smooth
diffeomorphism group has dense smooth vectors if it extends continuously
to a finite-Ck diffeomorphism group. CGD verifies the hypothesis with
k=6 for each actual descended circle representation. This density theorem
is an external analytic input, not an original CGD theorem or a native
certificate. The full 2001 PDF could not be retrieved in this pass;
access to its proof is not claimed. No unverified covering-group variant
is used: the central reduction produces the ordinary circle groups first.
