exact positive formula for every retained differentiated Taylor tail.
A finite polynomial transports by an exact composition law, while
the ledger keeps its original source and remainder until a fresh
complete source evaluation supplies a valid restart.

Fresh twenty-layer jets at s=31 and s=33, together with the retained
s=29 jet, give six overlapping whole-interval certificates. Both
actual 3x3 forms stay positive for every real s from 27 to 35; both
4x4 forms stay positive from 28 to 34. Ten-dimensional positivity
is separately checked at the three anchors. These statements use
uniform analytic bounds, not a grid of sample points.
