Primary analytic reference: <https://dlmf.nist.gov/1.9#E31>.
The exact tail uses the elementary differentiated geometric series;
finite polynomial transport follows from affine substitution and
the binomial theorem. No new priority for these classical facts,
positive moment theory or projection dilation is claimed.

The new adapter checks source metadata, degree and normalization;
this does not make arbitrary numeric arrays into actual xi data.
The verifier supplies its inputs only from the pinned LAT certificate
or fresh runs of the unchanged complete evaluator. These provenance
and analytic hypotheses are part of the certificate contract.
Likewise glue consumes the interval certificates produced by corridor;
an externally asserted certified flag is not an independent proof.

The independent proof audit checked the general-anchor bound,
positive differentiated tail, polynomial semigroup, retained-origin
error rule, no-reset example, and fixed-order atlas gluing. The audit
also identified the distinction between overlapping actual-source
charts and generally unequal rebuilt finite rational models; the
held-out source checks exercise that distinction.
