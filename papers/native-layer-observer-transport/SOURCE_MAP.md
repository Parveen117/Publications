The weighted metric, rational cut coordinates, nested rank-one update,
observer readout formula and wave-loss identities are derived in
THEOREM.md from these retained sources. This is a new integration in
this research track, not a claim of priority for orthogonal projection,
compression, change of Hilbert metric or canonical moment theory.

For the established canonical moment terminology and compact moment
coordinates, see Holger Dette and Jan Nagel, *Distributions on unbounded
moment spaces and random moment sequences*, Annals of Probability
40 (2012), 2690–2704, Section 2:
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.3369> and
<https://arxiv.org/pdf/1007.3369>.
LCW already rederived the required finite inverse identity. LOT uses
that identity rather than treating the canonical position as a newly
invented number or as a physical parameter.

For the actual completed xi source, ILC's all-order sign criterion is
the retained RH-equivalent obligation. LOT proves a conditional limit
given that premise; it does not prove RH or use RH as an unconditional
source fact. Physical interpretation is left open.
