| Source | Exact role |

The two-reflection wave is already present in ILC. LWP derives its
polynomial propagation and the different finite depth bands for states
and correlations, then connects those bands to a specified continuous
exponential. It does not introduce an independently chosen Hamiltonian
or identify the dimensionless parameter with physical time.

The scalar recurrence used for one-channel returns is the classical
Chebyshev recurrence. Primary references are NIST DLMF Table18.9.1,
<https://dlmf.nist.gov/18.9#T1>, and the explicit trigonometric
representations, <https://dlmf.nist.gov/18.5#i>. The recurrence is
rederived from the source wave in THEOREM.md and executed with native
rational coefficients. No priority claim is made for Chebyshev
polynomials, polynomial moment matching, or Taylor remainder bounds.

Finite model measures are used only as a proof representation of
already positive finite source forms. They are not identified with the
actual completed-source zero measure. The infinite source measure and
limit use the retained ILC all-order premise, whose RH-equivalent
actual-source sign obligation remains unresolved.
