Standard attribution: C. Weedbrook et al., *Gaussian Quantum Information*,
Reviews of Modern Physics 84, 621 (2012), arXiv:1110.3234v1,
sections II.2.6 and II.5.2:
https://arxiv.org/html/1110.3234v1 (accessed 16 October 2026).
Mixing a system with a vacuum probe and reading complementary output
quadratures is the established heterodyne/Gaussian POVM construction.
The kernel calculation in MDD-T3 fixes the source-specific normalization
directly; conventions in the review are not substituted for it.

Other standard inputs: the spectral/Stone theorems, Gaussian/Hermite
representation, density of cylinder functions, direct-integral
decomposability, the spectral representation of a cyclic vector,
Gaussian characteristic functions, Tonelli, and trace-class
approximation. The needed limit and domain arguments are supplied in
the theorem. No novel general measurement theorem is claimed.

Scientific additions relative to the parent: a source-compatible finite
mixing extension with actual feedback; a countable physical dilation
and induced instruments for CMG; a proof that the unchanged J-only
apparatus cannot realize the joint measurement; calibration of its
noise and its original source J moment; exact resolution-independent
smooth adapted records, distinguished from unstable point inversion.

Fable's residual-Diff ergodicity/rigging repair is a coordination task
on its separate branch, not a source or new proved dependency here.
