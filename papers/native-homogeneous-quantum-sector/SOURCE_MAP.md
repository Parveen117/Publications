Selected here, rather than inferred from the cut predicate: V=0 diagnostic;
canonical log-coordinate quantization with action unit one; regular-clock
test space excluding p=0; product constraint ordering; full-line reduced
Fourier representation and its matching across p=0. The operator, physical
norm, geometry representation and domain consequences are proved under
these choices. No uniqueness among all quantizations is claimed.

External mathematical methods are credited, not renamed as discoveries:

- D. Marolf, *Group Averaging and Refined Algebraic Quantization: Where are
  we now?*, arXiv:gr-qc/0011112 (2000),
  https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0011112. The general method constructs physical
  inner products for constrained systems. HQS derives its particular
  dp/|p| measure and excludes the singular product-constraint sector
  explicitly, rather than invoking a blanket averaging theorem.
- J. R. Klauder, *Completing Canonical Quantization, and Its Role in
  Nontrivial Scalar Field Quantization*, arXiv:1308.4658,
  https://arxiv.org/html/1308.4658v2. The relevant established idea is the
  positive-coordinate affine pair [Q,D]=i hbar Q and a self-adjoint
  dilation replacing an unsuitable ordinary momentum. No claims about
  other models or full gravity from that paper are used as premises here.

Sources inspected 16 October 2026. Standard Plancherel/Fourier theory,
self-adjoint real multiplication operators and the deficiency-index
criterion are the declared analytic tools. Their concrete domains,
unitary transforms, deficiency solutions and averaging Jacobian are
worked out in THEOREM.md. Finite matrix traces additionally obstruct a
strictly positive finite Hermitian realization of the exact affine relation.

HQS is distinct from AGD's existing global circle generator: it starts
from the REC restriction of LNC's actual constraint and constructs the
regular physical inner product. It does not replace AGD, solve the full
LNC local algebra or change the completed CHD manuscript's claims.
