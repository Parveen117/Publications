## PM1 and PM2: written general deductions

Let the native unit pair be `a + iota b`, with `a^2+b^2=1`, and let
`z=(a+iota b)^2`. Native multiplication gives the certified channel formulas.
For two unit readings, write their relative cut as `C+iota S`. The relation
`C^2+S^2=1` gives

\[
(C+\iota S)(1+C-\iota S)=1+C+\iota S.
\]

For `C != -1`, divide by `1+C` and use the native cut inverse to obtain
`C+iota S=(1+iota r)/(1-iota r)`, where `r=S/(1+C)`.
With the inherited phase chart
\(\Phi(r)=2\int_0^r (1+u^2)^{-1}du\), its transport increment is
\(\delta=\Phi(r)/2\in(-\pi/2,\pi/2)\).

Native circle periodicity says compatible transport increments differ by
`pi*n`, with integer `n`. Two increments in this strict open interval differ
in absolute value by less than `pi`, so `n=0`. This proves PM1 conditional on
the actual-step bound. A small observed representative does not establish that
bound. At the excluded endpoint `C=-1`, the increments `+pi/2` and `-pi/2`
produce the same channel.

Choose canonical phase representatives `phi_j` in `[0,pi)`. Their difference
lies in `(-pi,pi)`. If an actual step obeys the strict bound, exactly one
integer `k_j` places `phi_j-phi_(j-1)+pi*k_j` in the admitted interval; that
integer must be `-1`, `0` or `1`. The certified addition identity gives
`m_j=m_(j-1)+k_j`. Given a compatible anchor, PM1 fixes the first increment,
then the next. Induction fixes the entire sampled lift (PM2).

The executable quarter-step table stores rational coefficients of **symbolic
native pi**. The channel values are exact powers of `iota`; it does not use a

## AC1: divided identities and an admitted differential

Before imposing a unit constraint, the native compiler certifies

\[
X_0^2+Y_0^2=(a^2+b^2)^2,\qquad
X_0dY_0-Y_0dX_0=2(a^2+b^2)(a\,db-b\,da).
\]

It also certifies the reconstruction identities with
`h=a*da+b*db` and `t=a*db-b*da`:
`a*h-b*t=(a^2+b^2)*da` and `b*h+a*t=(a^2+b^2)*db`.
On a differentiable unit pair, `h=0`, hence `da=-b*t`, `db=a*t`.
The tangent derivation with precisely these images **passes the native
derivation gate**, so it preserves the unit-pair quotient. An arbitrary
choice `da=0, db=1` is rejected by that gate. This avoids differentiating a
quotient with an incompatible rule.

numerator `2*t`, `U^dagger dU=R*t`, and the trace coefficient. The gain

`N=X*dY-Y*dX` and `D=X^2+Y^2`. The `d rho` terms cancel. Dividing for nonzero
readings gives the stated current, including varying common positive gain.

## AC3: cancellation and its limits

For nonzero cut paths `u,v`, the certified identities are
`D[uv]=D[u]*D[v]` and `N[uv]=N[u]*D[v]+N[v]*D[u]`.
Division gives `Omega[uv]=Omega[u]+Omega[v]`. Dagger reverses the numerator

\[
\Omega[\rho_S g z_S]-\Omega[\rho_R g z_R]
=\Omega[z_S]-\Omega[z_R]
=\Omega[(\rho_S g z_S)(\rho_R g z_R)^\dagger]

for the specified shared nonzero factor and positive C1 gains. The last
equality does not require the magnitudes of the two gains to agree. Their
