\section{Conclusion}

A cut becomes mathematically useful only when it grades a declared carrier, a generator becomes physically useful only when its observer and target are typed, and a residual becomes a lawful ledger only when its carrier and transport rule are fixed before evaluation. Theorem 41 supplies the foundational cut-graded generator and bilateral algebra. Theorem 42 propagates the structure through a covariant response tower. Theorem 43 is the capstone synthesis that controls finite observed jets and target-recognition burden. Theorem 44 records the refinement ledger and its parity consequences rather than claiming that definitional closure alone is new physical evidence. The positive-metric cut-square theorem separates decoder-aligned response from transverse mismatch.

The nuclear adapter makes the cut concrete as neutron/proton response-axis exchange and produces a same-coverage guarded improvement on a frozen 3,558-nucleus chart. The electronic adapter makes the cut concrete as electron--hole or donor--acceptor exchange, derives the 118-position skeleton under explicit counting and ordering assumptions, and audits 92 frozen H--U ground configurations. The AI adapter makes the cut concrete as support/contradiction evidence exchange, preserves unsupported or uncertain material as an open residue, and demonstrates guarded commit, review, retrieval, regeneration, and block actions on frozen adversarial cases.

Together the three adapters support portability of the recognition governance pattern across physical response, occupancy, and information carriers. They do not prove that one microscopic or semantic operator governs all three domains. The result is deliberately less grandiose than a universal theory and more auditable than one: it is a theorem-governed recognition architecture with exact finite certificates, three source-bounded validation domains, explicit failures, source pins, and predeclared abstentions or guarded actions.

The decisive next experiments are a direct common-support benchmark of Recognition Repair against established mass models, temporal or independently held-out nuclear validation, a cross-fitted electronic model that predicts promotion residues without reading held-out configuration labels, and an independently authored multi-model AI benchmark with frozen outputs, exact adjudication, calibration, and selective-risk reporting.
