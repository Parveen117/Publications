\section{Synthesis and interpretation}

\subsection{What the theorem chain achieves}

Theorems 41--44 form one dependency-complete mathematical route:
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{cut and generator}
 \to \text{even/odd grading}
 \to \text{bilateral flow and operator cut square},\\
&\text{typed response tower}
 \to \text{nested recognition kernels}
 \to \text{finite reconstruction},\\
&\text{correction-to-tail transfer}
 \to \text{memory-closed or fail-closed classification}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Theorem 45 shows that this route can be consumed by a real validation pipeline without erasing its negative controls. The nuclear adapter preserves the direct-sum directional observation, distinguishes coherent and seam-stressed regimes, records open refinement, and refuses to promote a correction ratio that lacks error information.

\subsection{What is genuinely new in the combination}

The individual ingredients have standard neighbours: involutive gradings, operator exponentials, Taylor remainders, covariant jets, frame bounds, and guarded prediction. The contribution of the present synthesis is the exact interface between them:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*)]
\item the cut grades both the generator and every observed jet order;
\item bilateral flow supplies an internal covariance test before prediction;
\item recognition kernels identify the first derivative order at which a target becomes visible;
\item decoder burden separates visibility from quantitatively admissible recovery;
\item Madhava--Smriti refinement transfers a declared term from tail to body while preserving the recognized state;
\item the metric cut square resolves decoder-aligned and transverse response energy;
\item the nuclear adapter uses an observed seam to select between two lawful decoder regimes at unchanged coverage.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Why the negative results are load-bearing}

Several attempted promotions fail, and those failures sharpen the theory:
\begin{itemize}
\item a simple aggregate of directional observers can cancel even when direct-sum energy is nonzero;
\item adding jet depth does not guarantee monotone empirical error reduction for every nucleus;
\item the V6 fallback and order-selected point predictors do not outperform V5.1;
\item the strict V6 MAE improvement is caused by additional abstention, not new retained values;
\item the correction ratio has effectively zero correlation with the held-out error in the stressed sector;
\item a one-step fixed-space test can contain stroboscopic nonzero modes absent from the clock-free Eye.
\end{itemize}
A framework that records only successful examples is not a recognition system; it is a scrapbook.

\subsection{Unbounded and continuum extensions}

The bounded theory extends to an unbounded generator only after domain invariance, closability, and exponential generation are proved. A future theorem should specify a common core $\mathcal D$, establish $J\Dom(G)=\Dom(G)$, control $[\Ge,\Go]$ on the core or in form sense, and prove that the observed jet series converges in the chosen topology. None of these obligations is discharged by a finite nilpotent certificate.

Likewise, a continuum nuclear response bundle would require a declared state manifold or graph, a connection, physically typed response fibres, and an adapter from measured mass-table coordinates to the abstract cut and observer. The present finite validation is a useful constraint on such an adapter, not its derivation.
