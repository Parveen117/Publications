## 1. What would count as a result

the same local effect E_B in both cases, with no access to A's outcome labels.
Define the unconditional contrast

    Delta_E(s)=Pr(E_B | b=1,s)-Pr(E_B | b=0,s).             (1)

An information signal needs a nonzero contrast accessible to the receiver;
correlation or a postselected difference alone is insufficient. A physical
FTL claim additionally needs separated sender/receiver events and a contrast
before the earliest light arrival in the same physical geometry/protocol.
A reference light pulse that is merely delayed or follows a longer path is
not that benchmark. Shared event counters can order the comparison without
postulating time as a primitive, but still require an operational calibration.

The following finite models have no supplied physical separation or light
arrival. Therefore their contrasts have no measured propagation velocity.
The philosophical note in the canonical research record motivates this
direction but supplies no premise about consciousness or physical speed.
