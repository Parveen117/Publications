*The no-signaling condition and quantum dynamics*, Physical Review Letters 87,
170405 (2001), https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0102125
(read 15 October 2026). Their result explicitly depends on the quantum state,
probability and no-superluminal-communication assumptions. We do not invoke
their conclusion as an unconditional prohibition of every proposed theory.

the conditional nonlinear steering extension explicitly changes that contract.

No priority is claimed for remote steering, the general nonlinear-signalling
connection, local-channel no-signalling or the finite spectral norm bound.

are the addition to this repository's research chain.
