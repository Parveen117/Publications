and the meromorphic identity theorem are standard tools. No priority
claim is made for those general facts or for Euler's prime-reciprocal
divergence. POC-T4 supplies the elementary finite-Euler-product proof
needed for this specific sampling rate. A primary formal implementation
of prime-reciprocal divergence is the
[Mathlib archive](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Archive/Wiedijk100Theorems/SumOfPrimeReciprocalsDiverges.html);
this is attribution, not a claim that POC itself is Lean-verified.

[NIST DLMF 25.4](https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.4).
