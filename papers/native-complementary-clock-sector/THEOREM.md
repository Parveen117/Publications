# CCS-1 — Complementary clock sector and the two-axis physical completion

16 October 2026. Frozen predecessor: LSC-1 at 3e9f87c. Keep the smooth,
real, periodic V=0 matter-free LNC source, LQA reversed representation,
shared momenta P=2a_0 and K=2 pi b_0, and hbar=1. The aim is the physical
interpretation of the P=0 branch, not a new action or quantization of matter.

## 1. What P=0 means in the actual local classical equations

Use the unchanged definitions

    A_+=p+phi', A_-=p-phi', B_+=k+q', B_-=k-q',
    2T_+=A_+ B_+-A_+',   2T_-=A_- B_-+A_-'.

**CCS-T1 (smooth on-shell sector dichotomy).** Every smooth periodic
solution satisfies exactly one of the following:

1. P!=0: both A_+,A_- are nowhere zero, have the sign of P, and K=0.
   This is precisely the regular local reduction already treated by LPS-2.
2. P=0: A_+=A_-=0 identically, hence p=0 and phi(x)=Phi is constant.
   The smooth periodic functions q,k are unrestricted by the constraint
   values, and K=integral k may be zero or nonzero.

Proof. On shell the constraints are the linear ordinary differential
equations A_+'=B_+ A_+, A_-'=-B_- A_-. With smooth coefficients, their
solutions are A_epsilon(x)=A_epsilon(x_0) exp(epsilon integral B_epsilon).
Each current is either identically zero or nowhere zero with constant
sign. Periodicity gives integral A_+=integral A_-=P. A nowhere-zero
real current cannot have integral zero. Conversely an identically zero
one forces P=0 and therefore forces the other one identically zero too.
When P!=0 the periodic logarithmic derivatives have zero integral, so
integral B_+=integral B_-=K=0. On the second branch the original h,d
both vanish for every q,k because p=phi'=0.

This strengthens the earlier patch statement only **on smooth constraint
solutions**. Nonzero mean P alone, off shell, still does not rule out
zeros of A; LPS's off-shell control remains correct. No extension to
singular fields or nonsmooth coefficients is asserted.

## 2. Full local reduction on P=0, K!=0

On p=phi'=0, the actual Hamiltonian gauge transformations are

    delta q=N k+xi q'+xi',
    delta k=(N q')'+N''+(xi k)',
    delta Phi=0,      delta P=delta K=0.              (1)

Although the constraint values vanish on this branch, their Hamiltonian
vector fields do not. Therefore arbitrary q,k profiles cannot be counted
as independent physical degrees of freedom just from the zero values.

Set c_+=B_+=k+q', c_-=-B_-=q'-k. Under the two separate chiral flows,

    delta_+ c_+=(f c_+ +f')',  delta_+ c_-=0,
    delta_- c_-=-(f c_-+f')',  delta_- c_+=0.          (2)

Their means are kappa_+=K and kappa_-=-K. These are real affine-connection
transformation laws, rather than the density laws for the A currents.

**CCS-T2 (global affine straightening and geometry clock).** Every smooth
real periodic c of mean kappa is related to the constant kappa by a
connected orientation-preserving circle diffeomorphism. Consequently each
P=0,K!=0 gauge orbit has a representative

    p=0, phi=Phi, q=Q, k=K.                          (3)

Residual constant normal flow changes Q by N K while leaving Phi,K fixed.
The choice Q=tau is therefore a valid clock gauge, with reduced canonical
one-form K dPhi and reduced Hamiltonian zero.

Proof of straightening. Define W(x)=integral_0^x c and
Z(x)=integral_0^x exp(W(s)) ds. If kappa!=0, set on [0,1]

    F(x)=(1/kappa)log[1+(exp(kappa)-1)Z(x)/Z(1)].     (4)

The logarithm's argument is strictly positive. Its endpoint values are
1 and exp(kappa), so F(0)=0,F(1)=1; moreover F'>0, since
(exp(kappa)-1)/kappa>0. Direct differentiation gives

    c=kappa F'+F''/F'.                               (5)

Extending by F(x+1)=F(x)+1 is smooth, as follows from W(x+1)=W(x)+kappa
and the corresponding identity for Z. For kappa=0 the formula is instead
F=Z/Z(1), which satisfies c=F''/F'. These are exactly the finite affine
actions c -> (c composed with F)F'+F''/F' on the constant connection.
Their inverses straighten c. Interpolating the lift F with the identity
keeps its derivative positive, so the transformations are generated by
smooth time-dependent vector fields in (2), with the minus sign absorbed
into the second smearing. No quantum gauge-group representation is being
asserted by this classical construction.

Apply this independently to c_+,c_-; both means remain fixed and the two
chiral transformations commute. Then q'=0,k=K. The remaining additive q
is changed arbitrarily by residual constant lapse when K!=0, whereas Phi
is invariant under every local generator by p=phi'=0. Two representatives
with the same Phi,K and specified Q=tau coincide. Thus these variables
classify the quotient by the connected gauge flows on this stratum.
The pullback of integral(p dq+k dphi) is K dPhi. In the gauge-fixed action
the clock momentum P is zero, leaving no tau-Hamiltonian term.

Here Q=log(L/L_ref) on the homogeneous slice. Thus tau is a logarithmic
geometry clock and the relational circumference at that reading is

    L(tau)=L_ref exp(tau).                            (6)

Phi and K are tau-independent. This is a clock description of the selected
vacuum sector, not propagating matter, laboratory time or an energy spectrum.
The old scalar clock fails here because delta phi=Np=0; the geometry
clock is regular precisely because K!=0. At P=K=0 neither homogeneous
clock has nonzero clock bracket, so this reduction does not cover the origin.

## 3. A closed-form all-mode quantum state on the complementary branch

For one chirality use the positive creation variables U_n,V_n of LQA,
with u_-n=U_n,u_n=2n partial_(U_n), v_n=V_n,v_-n=2n partial_(V_n).
At P=0, u_0=b,w_0=-b with b=K/(2 pi). Define the formal vector

    I_0=exp[-sum_(n>0) U_n V_n/(2n)].                 (7)

The exponential means a formal coefficient series, or a functional on
finite oscillator tests. It is not a Fock-normalizable exponential.

**CCS-T3 (momentum-independent complementary dressing).** For every real K,

    (u_m+v_m) I_0=0,   L_m^rev I_0=0   for all m.     (8)

Its vacuum coefficient is one. By LSC's full kernel theorem it is the
unique normalized oscillator pairing at P=0. Both original chiralities
use the product I_0^+ tensor I_0^- and share the same K. In particular
the oscillator dressing has no K dependence to differentiate.

Proof. For n>0, differentiating the formal series gives
(2n partial_(U_n)+V_n)I_0=0 and (U_n+2n partial_(V_n))I_0=0; the
zero-mode sum is u_0+v_0=0. Every statement is coefficientwise finite
when paired with a test polynomial. In the equivalent w_n=v_-n positive
Fock coordinates these relations identify the transpose action of w with
minus u. Quadratic normal-ordered generators therefore have equal actions
T_m(u)I_0=T_-m(w)I_0; the number terms at m=0 cancel, including the
equal squared zero modes. The original improvement is i m(u_m+v_m)/2
and vanishes by the first identities. This proves the full constraints,
without dropping the improvement or assigning a finite norm to (7).

Explicitly, the linear bra R_0 on a monomial U^r V^s vanishes unless
the occupation arrays r,s agree; if they do, its value is

    product_(n>0) (-1)^(r_n) (2n)^(r_n) r_n!.         (9)

This proves finite-test continuity directly. It also shows why the plain
oscillator vacuum is not an alternative physical state. The norm squared
of each paired level is p(level); the infinite sum diverges as in LPS.
The two equations a_m^epsilon I=0 imply p and phi' vanish as distributional
current modes in this sector, consistently with the classical reduction.

## 4. Positive physical norm and genuine observables

Use the dense invariant test space

    Phi_K=C_c^infinity(R_P x (R\{0})_K) tensor_alg F_fin.

Let R_K f(K)=<I_0^+ tensor I_0^-, f(0,K)>_osc. It maps continuously and
surjectively onto C_c^infinity(R\{0}), by vacuum tests, and R_K L_m^epsilon=0.
Choose the same normalization of dP dK and the same one-constraint average
as HQS/LPS, now in the K!=0 chart. The finite-test off-shell contraction
T_K f(P,K)=<I_0^+ tensor I_0^-,f(P,K)>_osc is smooth and compactly supported.

**CCS-T4 (positive complementary physical completion).** The declared
contraction followed by the average of exp(i s P K) gives

    B_K(f,g)=integral ds/(2 pi) <T_K f, exp(i s P K) T_K g>
            =integral_(K!=0) dK/|K| (R_K f)^* R_K g.  (10)

Its null quotient completion is

    H_K=L^2(R\{0},dK/|K|).                            (11)

Proof. Integration by parts in P gives arbitrary inverse powers of s
since the compact K support stays away from zero. Fourier inversion,
or the positive Gaussian approximate identity with y=KP, gives the
absolute Jacobian 1/|K|. Positivity, null space ker R_K and dense/surjective
test range prove (11). The all-mode dual state associated with chi in H_K
is eta_chi[f]=integral (R_K f)^* chi dK/|K|, continuous by Cauchy-Schwarz.
This is a positive physical norm; it is not the divergent Fock norm of (7).
As in LPS, the prescription is oscillator contraction followed by scalar
averaging, not a Haar average over the full local gauge group.

The classical reduction in T2 now provides the interpretation of its
observables. Select the same full-line Fourier matching convention as HQS,
with J_K chi=chi/sqrt(|K|) and F_Phi the Fourier transform for (Phi,K).
Let A_K=F_Phi^-1 J_K. Then

    K_phys chi=K chi,  Dom K_phys={chi:K chi in H_K},
    Phi_phys=J_K^-1(i partial_K)J_K,
    Dom Phi_phys=J_K^-1 H^1(R),
    Phi_phys chi=i[chi'-chi/(2K)] away from zero.       (12)

These are self-adjoint by the same real-multiplication/Fourier proof as
HQS. On A_K^-1 C_c^infinity(R_Phi), [Phi_phys,K_phys]=iI. The full-line
matching remains selected, not forced by the disconnected classical
K!=0 strata. LSC's conditional measure theorem applies with P replaced
by K: fixed expression (12) and matching fix dK/|K| up to normalization.

The scalar field evaluated on the complete gauge slice is Phi_phys at
every spatial point; k is K_phys on that uniform slice. The geometry
clock observables are q(tau)=tau I, L(tau)=L_ref exp(tau) I and p(tau)=0.
The latter length is a positive scalar operator with full Hilbert domain.
They are quantizations of the full local model's relational gauge-slice
observables, not arbitrary labels on a Hilbert space. All are independent
of tau except the specified clock reading itself; the reduced Hamiltonian
is zero. The construction does not require a pointwise kinematic exp(q(x)).

On compact regular tests the quotient action of the scalar observable is
also explicit: R_K[i partial_K f-i f/(2K)] = Phi_phys R_K f. There is no
derivative of the oscillator dressing because (7) is K independent. The
measure correction is still essential: bare i partial_K is not symmetric
for dK/|K|. This is a quotient/test intertwiner, not a claim that the corrected
test operator is symmetric in the original unweighted kinematic norm.

## 5. One averaging prescription for both noncritical branches

Use LSC's continuous smooth scalar reduction rho, now in P,K coordinates,
on

    Phi_cross=C_c^infinity(R^2\{(0,0)}) tensor_alg F_fin.

Its restrictions to K=0 and P=0 are respectively the LPS and CCS paired
bras, normalized on the vacuum. Define T f=rho f, R_P f(P)=rho f(P,0)
and R_K f(K)=rho f(0,K).

**CCS-T5 (two-axis physical Hilbert space away from the origin).**

    B_cross(f,g)=integral ds/(2 pi)<rho f,exp(i s P K)rho g>
      =integral dP/|P| (R_P f)^*R_P g
        +integral dK/|K| (R_K f)^*R_K g,               (13)
    H_cross=H_P direct_sum H_K.                      (14)

Proof. On every compact support away from the origin the gradient of PK
is (K,P), nowhere zero. Repeated integration by parts with the smooth
vector field (K partial_P+P partial_K)/(P^2+K^2), including its divergence
in the adjoint, makes the scalar oscillatory integral rapidly decreasing
in s. A smooth partition into charts where P!=0 or K!=0, followed by
the respective one-variable delta/Jacobian calculation, gives (13).
The two axis pieces have disjoint support on the punctured plane, so no
intersection term is counted twice. Vacuum tests supported near compact
subintervals of either open axis independently realize arbitrary compact
smooth amplitudes there. Thus their joint range is dense in the direct
sum and its null quotient completion is precisely (14).

Both weights have coefficient one because we selected one common scalar
average, one dP dK normalization and vacuum-normalized contractions.
This is not a proof that the constraints alone impose their relative weight.
The resulting physical momentum operators act as (P,0) and (0,K) on the
two summands. The branch projections are bona fide orthogonal physical
observables. They are superselected only relative to a declared block-
diagonal observable algebra; no theorem forbids all possible operators
mixing the two solution spaces. The clocks are branch dependent and their
canonical pairs must not be silently applied to the other summand.

## 6. The joint origin is a real boundary, not a discarded solution

**CCS-T6 (critical-average obstruction and optional origin line).** The
positive Gaussian-regularized version of (13) diverges on any smooth
vacuum test whose scalar value at (0,0) is nonzero as the regulator is
removed. Thus this same prescription has no finite extension to all of
Phi_all. Separately,

    B_0(f,g)=c conjugate(rho f(0,0))rho g(0,0), c>0,  (15)

is a positive constraint-annihilating form with one-dimensional completion.
It is not the value of the divergent average and does not supply a regular
clock or canonical geometry/scalar observable pair at the origin.

Proof of divergence. The average of exp(i s P K-epsilon s^2) is the
positive kernel (4 pi epsilon)^(-1/2) exp[-P^2 K^2/(4 epsilon)]. If the
scalar test is nonzero at the origin, its squared modulus is bounded below
by a positive constant on a small square |P|,|K|<delta. In the region
sqrt(epsilon)/delta<P<delta and |K|<sqrt(epsilon)/P, the kernel is bounded
below by exp(-1/4)/sqrt(4 pi epsilon). Integrating K gives a positive
constant times 1/P; the remaining integral grows logarithmically without
bound. No cancellation is available in this positive quadratic form.

Equivalently the measures dP/|P| and dK/|K| are not locally finite at the
joint origin. A full-test-space positive form compatible with scalar
momentum multipliers would be a locally finite positive measure (LSC's
argument), so it cannot restrict to these unchanged weights and include
all nonvanishing-origin tests. Changing the test domain, observable contract
or renormalization would be an additional quantization choice.

For (15), LSC gives rho(L_m f)=PK times a smooth function, up to the
irrelevant constant 4 pi. Evaluation at the origin annihilates it.
Positivity and rank one are immediate. It retains only the scalar origin
evaluation; the higher distribution jets in LSC are not thereby assigned
a positive norm. Both physical momenta act as zero on this line, so neither
can have a canonical conjugate with commutator iI there. This agrees with
the failure of both classical homogeneous clock brackets at P=K=0.
A geometric interpretation or coupling of an origin sector to the two
regular clock sectors remains additional work.

## 7. Endpoint and certificate scope

The previously scalar-clock-singular branch P=0,K!=0 now has a full local
classical reduction, a geometry clock, an explicit all-mode oscillator
solution, a positive physical inner product and self-adjoint reduced scalar
observables with domains. The two noncritical axes are covered by one
specified positive averaging construction. Their physical norms do not
come from treating generalized vectors as normalizable kinematic states.

Open: physical selection/gluing at the critical origin, quantum relational
fields beyond the specified gauge-slice construction, nonzero potential,
quantized matter, global cut gluing and physical 3+1 gravity. A clock change
has not produced interacting dynamics or a calibrated new prediction.

General ODE, affine-diffeomorphism, distributional and operator-domain
arguments are proved in writing. Native exact checks separately cover
gauge variations, formal squeezed-state coefficients, all-mode actions on
finite tests, signed-Jacobian and domain controls, and the origin obstruction's
algebraic ingredients. No finite truncation is called an all-mode state.
