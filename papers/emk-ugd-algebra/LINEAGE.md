# Lineage — EMK / UGD algebra

## Origin

The EMK algebraic core ladder (I–VII) and the UGD algebraic-operators
appendix exist in the provisional vault as LaTeX: definitions, theorems
and proofs, written and reviewed but never executed. A search of the
public Recognition-Kernel-Framework and the RH-Framework found **no UGD
algebra implementation** in either. EMK-1 is therefore the first
machine-checkable realization of this layer, not a port of existing code.

## What EMK-1 adds

Nothing mathematically new is claimed. What is added is execution and
sharpening:

- the additive determinant identity verified on the full rational
  coefficient grid by two independent routes;
- **channel independence** exhibited explicitly (each determinant channel
  vanishing alone; both vanishing on a nonzero operator) — turning the
  ladder's "channels can close or fail independently" from a remark into
  a certificate;
- the incompleteness of first-order closure witnessed by an operator with
  unit diagonal-block determinants and a nonzero quadratic residue;
- the winding obstruction made exact through a rational circle
  parametrization and integer crossing counts, with no trigonometry and
  no floats anywhere in the verdict path;
- an exact algebra-level bridge to Cut-First Equivalence (T7).

## Relationship to the other capsules

- **Cut-First Equivalence** (`../cut-first-equivalence/`): T7 identifies
  the CFE memory dial with the EMK rotational determinant channel, so
  CFE's explicit rational equation of state can be replaced by this
  native algebra. That replacement is the next capsule's work, not a
  claim made here.
- **Λ-seam calibration** (`../lambda-seam-calibration/`): the quarter turn
  R²=−I certified here is the same quarter turn as LAM-2's derived seam
  phase ι²=−1 and CFE-Q's Bloch generator J²=−I — three presentations,
  one object.

## Claim boundary

Certified: the finite-dimensional spine. Deferred by the source and not
asserted here: all infinite-dimensional analytic extensions. Untouched:
RH / K₀ / L₀ / YM continuum gates, and quantum gravity.

## EMK-2 addendum

EMK-2 consumes EMK-1's T7 licence and retires the scaffold equation of
state used by the Cut-First Equivalence capsules. Two things are added.

First, the ℤ/2 grading is made explicit and certified: the even (seam)
sector {I,K} and the odd (rotational) sector {R,RK}, with the sector
multiplication laws. EMK-1's determinant channels read exactly these two
grades, so the memoryless/memory split is the grading itself.

Second, the framework's native multiplicative-to-additive law is bound to
the seam channel. The derivation of Log_Sigma lives in the public
Recognition-Kernel-Framework (theorems/foundation/F00G, Theorem 7.1) and
is a PINNED NAMED DEPENDENCY here — cited, never rederived. What EMK-2
certifies is the exact Cayley-chart identity: even-sector composition is
the rational addition law (y1+y2)/(1+y1y2), which under
x = (1+y)/(1-y) is exactly Log_Sigma(x1 x2) = Log_Sigma(x1) +
Log_Sigma(x2). No series, no transcendental evaluation, no floats.

The retirement is certified to be free: under rho = chi - 1 the scaffold
and native curvature densities coincide identically, and the native
residues reproduce CFE-1's certified values (-32, -16, 0, +16, +32)
exactly. CFE's numbers did not depend on the equation of state; the
equation of state was a coordinate on the odd grade.

## UGD-1 addendum

UGD-1 certifies the UGD number layer: cut-zero, operator numerals, and
the lambda-paraconsistent structural logic. As with EMK-1 and EMK-2, the
source is vault LaTeX and no executable version existed.

Two representation decisions keep the capsule exact. Phase is carried as
an EXPONENT in Z/K and never evaluated as a root of unity; the scale base
is an exact rational. Consequently no transcendental arithmetic enters
any verdict path, and the phase algebra is certified as the cyclic group
it is rather than as a numerical approximation of one.

The sharpest results are the carry conservation law (seam charge pushed
out of the balanced alphabet is conserved in the ledger, with a clamping
control that loses charge and separates) and the projection-blindness
witness (numerals sharing a classical value while differing in seam data
and total seam charge). Together they make precise the appendix's
principle that visible equality is not UGD equality.

Not claimed: convergence of admissibly infinite numerals, and the seam
composition rule for linked seams, which the source declares rather than
derives. Both are recorded as such in the certificate.
