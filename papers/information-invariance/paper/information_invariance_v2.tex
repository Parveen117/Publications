\title{Information Invariance as a Selection Principle:\\
Proved Finite Structure, and Conjectured Emergent Dynamics}

\author{Monty Dabas}
\affiliation{Celextrix Private Limited, New Delhi 110086, India}
\email{montydabas@celextrix.org}

\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
We give a concrete definition of information invariance for thermodynamic
response charts: the stationarity of the loop functional of the entropy-scaled
accessibility one-form. On a finite chart we prove two statements. First,
information invariance is \emph{equivalent} to vanishing thermodynamic
curvature and to exactness of the one-form; on the $\lambda$-chart this is
precisely the Onsager-reciprocal (flat) sector. Second, the loop area measured
on a \emph{single} thermal cycle does not witness curvature: a flat chart with a
one-time irreversible offset and a curved chart can produce identical
first-cycle areas, and only the second cycle separates them. Both statements are
accompanied by executable certificates over exact rational arithmetic. We then
state, explicitly as conjectures rather than results, a null-coordinate energy
dynamics compatible with the principle, together with the emergent-quantum,
emergent-gravity and gravitational-decoherence claims that a full derivation would have to
establish; each carries a named open obligation. For the quantum step we carry
the analysis to a conclusion: we prove
the precise structural criterion --- a real linear flow is a Schr\"odinger flow
exactly when it carries a complex structure with an antisymmetric commuting
generator --- and prove that a single real channel never satisfies it, which
reduces that obligation to two explicit requirements, one of which is then
discharged by the derived quarter turn and cut grading of the recognition
framework; performing the remaining computation shows that the proposed energy
dynamics fails the criterion, so the emergent-quantum step is withdrawn as a
claim and retained only as an explicit conditional. We correct the associated decoherence formula, which as printed is not a rate,
and identify its repair with the Di\'osi--Penrose expression, so that it is a
consistency requirement rather than a new prediction; and we state which of our
own Raman measurements currently do and do not support the framework.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Onsager reciprocity is derived under microscopic reversibility. Entropy-weighted
reparameterisations of response functions yield an effective coupling matrix
whose cross-coefficients are generically asymmetric~\cite{Dabas2026}; the
associated geometric statement is that the accessibility one-form
$\omega=\lambda_p\,dp+\lambda_v\,dv$ need not be closed. This raises a
structural question: \emph{which} irreversible trajectories are admissible.

The present paper separates what can be proved from what is presently
conjectured. Section~\ref{sec:def} defines information invariance as a loop
functional. Section~\ref{sec:proved} proves two finite theorems about it, with
machine-checked certificates. Section~\ref{sec:empirical} states the empirical
situation honestly, including a non-closure finding in our own data that
weakens the naive reading of loop area. Section~\ref{sec:conj} states the
dynamical programme as conjectures with explicit obligations.

\section{Information invariance, defined}\label{sec:def}

\begin{definition}[Information invariance]\label{def:ii}
Let $\omega$ be the entropy-scaled accessibility one-form on a response chart.
Define the loop functional $\cI(\gamma)=\oint_\gamma\omega$ for closed paths
$\gamma$. The chart is \emph{information invariant} if $\cI(\gamma)=0$ for every
admissible closed $\gamma$, written $\delta\cI=0$.
\end{definition}

This is a selection principle: it constrains admissible evolution, and asserts
neither conservation of microscopic information nor invariance under arbitrary
transformations. It postulates no new interaction and does not quantise
spacetime.

\section{Proved finite structure}\label{sec:proved}

Discretise the chart on a lattice with rational edge values; $\Omega=d\omega$ is
the plaquette circulation, and discrete Stokes holds exactly.

\begin{theorem}[Information invariance $=$ flat sector]\label{thm:A}
On a finite simply connected chart the following are equivalent:
(i) $\delta\cI=0$; (ii) $\Omega\equiv0$; (iii) $\omega$ is exact, i.e.\ a
potential $\Phi$ exists and line integrals are path independent. On the
$\lambda$-chart with $\lambda_X=-ST/C_X$, (ii) is the flat-closure sector
$\Gamma_c\Gamma_m=1$, i.e.\ Onsager reciprocity.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
(ii)$\Rightarrow$(i) by discrete Stokes; (i)$\Rightarrow$(ii) by taking
$\gamma=\partial$(plaquette); (i)$\Leftrightarrow$(iii) is path independence
$\Leftrightarrow$ exactness on a connected graph. All three directions, plus
planted-curvature negative controls, are executed over exact rationals in the
accompanying certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One cycle does not witness curvature]\label{thm:B}
There exist a flat chart with a single irreversible offset and a curved chart
without offset whose first-cycle loop areas are equal. The two are separated
only by the second cycle: the offset construction collapses (reopen ratio $0$),
the curvature construction reopens with the same area (ratio $1$).
\end{theorem}

Consequently a nonzero loop area measured on one cycle is consistent with
irreversible drift and does not by itself establish $\iint\Omega\neq0$.
Certificates for both theorems are in
\texttt{papers/information-invariance/certificates}; the pinned SHA-256 is
reproduced by continuous integration on every commit.

\section{Empirical situation}\label{sec:empirical}

Temperature-cycled Raman measurements on monolayer CVD graphene on
SiO$_2$~\cite{Dabas2026} give reproducible first-cycle hysteresis in the $G$ and
$2D$ peak positions, with loop areas well above fit uncertainty (independently
re-fitted: $744\pm22$ and $692\pm28$\,cm$^{-1}$K at spot~1). The loop, however,
does \emph{not} close at $300$\,K: the cooling branch returns $+4.95$\,cm$^{-1}$
($G$, spot~1) above the heating branch. By Theorem~\ref{thm:B} this first-cycle
observation is compatible with an irreversible component (adsorbate desorption
or strain release) and does not establish reversible curvature. A predeclared
decision rule has been registered: reopen ratio $\geq0.6$ on both bands supports
curvature; $\leq0.2$ indicates irreversible drift~\cite{ThermoRepro}. The
measurement is pending.

\section{Conjectured dynamics and its obligations}\label{sec:conj}

The following are stated as conjectures. Each names the obligation a derivation
must discharge; none is used to support the claims of Sec.~\ref{sec:proved}.

\begin{conjecture}[Null-coordinate realisation]\label{conj:dyn}
With $u=ct-r$ and energy density $\cE(u,\theta,\phi)$, an admissible local
realisation of $\delta\cI=0$ takes the form
\begin{equation}
\partial_u\cE=\alpha_G\cE^2+\alpha_\Lambda\cE_{\rm vac}+\epsilon\,\mathcal{F}[\cE].
\end{equation}
\textbf{Obligation D1:} derive this form \emph{from} Definition~\ref{def:ii}
rather than positing it; in particular show which variational problem
$\delta\cI=0$ makes $\alpha_G\cE^2$ the unique quadratic term.
\end{conjecture}

\subsection{Where a complex phase can come from (proved)}

The step usually left implicit is the appearance of a complex phase in a real
theory. Its finite content is settled.

\begin{theorem}[Real flow $=$ Schr\"odinger flow]\label{thm:C}
Let $x'=Ax$ be a real linear flow on $\mathbb{R}^{2n}$ and $J$ an orthogonal
complex structure ($J^2=-I$, $J^{T}=-J$). Then $A^{T}=-A$ and $AJ=JA$ if and
only if $H:=-\hbar JA$ is Hermitian for $J$, in which case the flow reads
$i\hbar\psi'=H\psi$ under $x\leftrightarrow\psi$; equivalently, the flow is
complex linear and norm preserving.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[One-channel obstruction]\label{thm:D}
An odd-dimensional real flow admits no complex structure, since
$\det(J)^2=\det(-I)=(-1)^N$. In particular a single scalar channel
$x'=ax$ is never a Schr\"odinger flow, for any $a$ and any rescaling.
\end{theorem}

Linearising Conjecture~\ref{conj:dyn} about a background gives, at leading
order, the single real channel
$\partial_u\delta\cE=2\alpha_G\bar\cE\,\delta\cE+\epsilon\,\mathcal{F}'\delta\cE$.
By Theorem~\ref{thm:D} this cannot be Schr\"odinger evolution as it stands, and
by Theorem~\ref{thm:C} what a derivation must supply is explicit:

\begin{itemize}
\item[\textbf{D2a}] a conjugate channel $\pi$ of $\delta\cE$ within the dynamics,
and a complex structure $J$ on the pair $(\delta\cE,\pi)$;
\item[\textbf{D2b}] antisymmetry of the linearised generator on that pair, i.e.\
the quadratic self-interaction must contribute to the Hermitian $H$ and not to a
symmetric part of $A$.
\end{itemize}

The first is supplied by structure that is already derived, not assumed. An
oriented cut yields a quarter turn $\iota$ with $\iota^2=-1$ and
$\iota^\dagger=-\iota$~\cite{RKF-F00E}, and every generator splits uniquely
relative to the cut $J$ into an even part preserving the two sheets and an odd
part transporting between them~\cite{RKF-41}. Taking the conjugate channel to
be the image on the opposite sheet gives:

\begin{theorem}[Cut supplies the pair; phase is the odd part]\label{thm:E}
Let $J$ be a balanced cut, and write the generator in the sheet basis as blocks
$A=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}A_{++}&B\\ C&A_{--}\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$ with
$\iota=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}0&-I\\ I&0\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$. Then
$A\iota=\iota A$ iff $A_{++}=A_{--}$ and $C=-B$; $A^{T}=-A$ iff the diagonal
blocks are antisymmetric and $C=-B^{T}$; both hold iff the diagonal blocks are
equal and antisymmetric and $B$ is symmetric, in which case $H=-\hbar\iota A$ is
Hermitian and Theorem~\ref{thm:C} applies. A purely cut-even generator never
mixes the sheets and produces no phase, and the symmetric part of $A$ is exactly
the damping: $d|x|^2/du=2x^{T}A_{\rm sym}x$.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{The computation, and its negative outcome}

Theorem~\ref{thm:E} leaves one computation. We perform it on a transverse
lattice with the parity cut, in exact arithmetic. The linearisation of
Conjecture~\ref{conj:dyn} about a background contributes three operator types:
pointwise multiplication $M$ by $2\alpha_G\bar\cE$ from the quadratic term, a
second difference $L$ from any diffusive part of $\mathcal F$, and a first
difference $D$ from any transport part. We find:

\begin{itemize}
\item $M$ is symmetric and cut even; it contributes $d|x|^2/du=4\alpha_G\bar\cE|x|^2$,
i.e.\ pure growth or decay, and no phase;
\item $L$ is symmetric and negative semidefinite: diffusion, no phase;
\item $D$ is antisymmetric and cut odd: it alone preserves the norm and meets
the conditions of Theorem~\ref{thm:E};
\item for $A=M+L+D$ the symmetric part is exactly $M+L$, which does not vanish
at a nonzero background; a coordinate rescaling is a similarity and cannot
remove it.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Result.} The linearised dynamics of
Conjecture~\ref{conj:dyn} is \emph{not} a Schr\"odinger generator. It is a
damped (or amplified) rotation. The emergent-quantum step therefore does not
follow from the equation as written, and we withdraw it as a claim. What
survives is a sharp conditional: the step holds if and only if the dynamics is
amended so that the cut-even symmetric part cancels at the background,
$M+L=0$, leaving the cut-odd transport term as the generator. Theorems~\ref{thm:A}
and~\ref{thm:B} are independent of this and are unaffected.

\begin{conjecture}[Emergent geometry]\label{conj:grav}
In the adiabatic limit the induced metric satisfies the Einstein equations, in
the sense of~\cite{Jacobson1995}. \textbf{Obligation D3$'$:} derive this rather
than citing the analogy. This half remains open.
\end{conjecture}

\subsection{The regulator scale (settled)}

The finiteness claim usually attached to Conjecture~\ref{conj:grav} is
$\langle\cE(x)\cE(x')\rangle\sim(r^2+L_P^2)^{-1}$ with
$L_P=\sqrt{\hbar G/c^3}$. Dimensional analysis decides its status.

\begin{theorem}[No Planck length without $\hbar$]\label{thm:F}
In the $(M,L,T)$ dimension group, $\hbar$ is not in the span of $\{c,G\}$, and no
product $G^ac^b$ is a length. Hence $L_P$ cannot be constructed from the
deterministic constants; admitting it presupposes $\hbar$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The theory's own regulator is cosmological]\label{thm:G}
From $\{c,G,\cE\}$ a length does exist, uniquely up to powers:
$L_\cE=c^2/\sqrt{G\cE}$, equivalently $\Lambda^{-1/2}$. For a vacuum energy
density of the observed order, $L_\cE/L_P\sim10^{61}$.
\end{theorem}

Consequently the model regularises, if at all, at a cosmological scale;
inserting $L_P$ is an assumption and the ultraviolet-finiteness claim of the
earlier draft is withdrawn. The theory must either admit $\hbar$ as a primitive
--- forfeiting the claim to derive quantum structure --- or work with
$L_\cE$.

\subsection{The decoherence rate (corrected)}

\begin{theorem}[The printed rate is not a rate]\label{thm:H}
$Gm^2/(\hbar V)$ has dimensions $M^0L^{-2}T^{-1}$: a rate per unit area. The
unique repair replacing the volume by a length, $Gm^2/(\hbar R)$, is
dimensionally a rate and is exactly the Di\'osi--Penrose expression
$E_G/\hbar$ with $E_G\sim Gm^2/R$~\cite{Diosi1987,Penrose1996}.
\end{theorem}

With $m=10^{-17}$\,kg and $R=V^{1/3}=10^{-7}$\,m the repaired rate is
$6.3\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, of the order quoted in the earlier draft ---
but it is a Di\'osi--Penrose number, recovered only after the correction. We
therefore state the result as a \emph{consistency requirement}: any emergent
gravity of this kind must reproduce $E_G/\hbar$ in this regime. It becomes a
prediction only if one of the following is supplied: a derivation of the
coefficient, which DP fixes only through a choice of regularisation length; a
mass or geometry regime of departure; or a dependence on shape or entropy
gradient, which DP does not carry. Optomechanical tests discriminate such
departures~\cite{Aspelmeyer2014,Bassi2013}, not the agreement.

\section{Conclusion}

Information invariance, defined as stationarity of the loop functional, is
equivalent on finite charts to vanishing thermodynamic curvature and to
exactness --- a proved statement with an executable certificate. The
experimental signature usually quoted for curvature, a nonzero one-cycle loop
area, is proved insufficient; the decision is deferred to a predeclared
second-cycle test. The dynamical programme connecting this principle to quantum and gravitational
phenomena is stated as conjectures with named obligations. For the quantum step
the obligation is discharged with a negative outcome: the proposed energy
dynamics generates a damped rotation rather than unitary evolution, and the
step survives only as the explicit condition that the cut-even symmetric part
cancel at the background. We regard reporting this as more useful than
asserting the step.
