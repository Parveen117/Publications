## Attribution and reuse

Coherent-state/anti-Wick quantization and Gaussian joint measurements
are established tools. The countable isometry and balanced apparatus
were already constructed in CMG/MDD. WCG adapts their covariance-independent
arguments to the existing LMD sector and derives the Wick-mass calibration.

See Biswajit Chakraborty, Jean Pierre Gazeau and Ahmed Youssef,
*Coherent state quantization of angle, time, and more irregular functions
and distributions*, [author manuscript, arXiv:0805.1847v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.1847v2),
accessed 16 October 2026. MDD's source map also credits the Gaussian
heterodyne construction. No new general coherent-state quantization
or measurement theorem is claimed here.

The Gaussian multiplicative-chaos construction and its established
attribution are inherited from LMD. Its elementary L2 proof applies
at the detector parameter 2h only when 2h<1. WCG does not infer that
no other construction exists outside this sufficient range.

Conditional expectation in L2, Tonelli, Gaussian convolution, heat-kernel
smoothing, Sobolev composition, positive closed forms and the continuous
mapping theorem are standard tools. Their needed estimates and domain
arguments are displayed in THEOREM.md.
