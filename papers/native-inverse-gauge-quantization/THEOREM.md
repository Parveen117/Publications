## 1. Exact source embeddings, not an unrestricted gauge-chart replacement

Use MPQ's canonical source coordinates (c_m,m),(phi,K_phi),(Z,R_Z).
Here m(z) has one nonzero sign, P=integral m!=0, p=m(Z)Z',
v=phi', Z'>0, |v|<|p|. Define the fixed periodic primitive R by

    (R f)'=f-integral f, integral R f=0,
    M_m(z)=z+(R m)(z)/P, M_m'=m/P>0.                    (1)

The mean-zero primitive avoids a discontinuous functional derivative at
an arbitrarily chosen material base point. M_m is a degree-one lift.

**IGQ-T1 (complete embedding chart and source moment maps).** Put

    F_+(x)=M_m(Z(x))+phi(x)/P,
    F_-(x)=M_m(Z(x))-phi(x)/P.                            (2)

Both F_epsilon are increasing degree-one diffeomorphism lifts, with
F_epsilon'=A_epsilon/P>0. Conversely, arbitrary such F_+,F_- and any
regular m give

    Z=M_m^{-1}((F_++F_-)/2),
    phi=P(F_+-F_-)/2, p=m(Z)Z'.                         (3)

The pair of lifts is identified under a common integer addition, since
Z is a circle map while phi is real. This retains the relative real
zero mode; it does not replace two lifts by two independent circle maps.
On the full regular source, not only on the constraint surface,

    delta_epsilon m=0,
    delta_epsilon F_epsilon=epsilon f F_epsilon',
    delta_epsilon F_(-epsilon)=0.                        (4)

The cotangent lift of (2) gives canonical embedding momenta

    Pi_+=(P/2)K_phi+[P/(2m(Z))]R_Z,
    Pi_-=-(P/2)K_phi+[P/(2m(Z))]R_Z.                     (5)

The actual chiral constraint densities are exactly

    T_tilde_+=F_+' Pi_+, T_tilde_-=-F_-' Pi_-.            (6)

Thus the original regular gauge action is independent right composition
on F_+ and F_-, with the inherited opposite chiral signs. In the inverse
embeddings chi_epsilon=F_epsilon^{-1}, it is left composition, with
delta_epsilon chi_epsilon=-epsilon f composed with chi_epsilon.
These finite actions preserve monotonicity and the source patch.

Proof. MRF's off-shell transformations are delta p=(V p)',
delta Z=V Z', V=epsilon f A_epsilon/(2p), and
delta phi=f A_epsilon/2. The inverse-coordinate variation cancels the
transport of p/Z', so m and P are invariant even off shell. Applying
these formulas to (2) proves (4). Equations (2),(3) are smooth inverses;
their derivative inequalities give exactly the regular source conditions.

At fixed m, delta phi=(P/2)(delta F_+-delta F_-) and
delta Z=P(delta F_++delta F_-)/(2m(Z)). Inserting these into
K_phi delta phi+R_Z delta Z gives (5). Since
H_tilde=p K_phi+(v/p)Z'R_Z and D_tilde=v K_phi+Z'R_Z,
(6) follows directly. There is no discarded improvement or added
configuration-only term: MPQ's exact source canonical map already
absorbs the original derivative improvement into K_phi and c_m.

There is an essential off-shell correction to the physical conjugate.
Modulo an exact one-form, the old potential is
-integral c_m delta m+integral(K_phi delta phi+R_Z delta Z).
Define B by

    integral B delta m dz
         =integral [Pi_+ delta_m F_+ +Pi_- delta_m F_-]dx. (7)

Then c_bar=c_m+B, m, F_epsilon,Pi_epsilon are canonical. B is smooth
and linear in the gauge momenta. Explicitly set

    h(z)=[(Pi_++Pi_-)/Z'](Z^{-1}(z)),
    C=integral [(Pi_++Pi_-)(R m)(Z)
                            +(Pi_+-Pi_-)phi]dx.
    B(z)=-(R h)(z)/P-C/P^2.                             (8)

Indeed delta_m F_epsilon=(R delta m)(Z)/P
-[(R m)(Z)+epsilon phi]delta P/P^2 and R^*=-R; these give (8).
It is incorrect to leave c_m unchanged off shell under the point
transformation (2). On Pi_+=Pi_-=0, c_bar=c_m exactly.

The source probe has a strongly invariant representative

    G_w^*=integral m w a(c_bar+log|P|), a(t)=1+cos(t)/2.  (9)

Its difference from the original G_w, with c_m instead of c_bar, lies
in the smooth ideal generated by Pi_+,Pi_-. This follows by writing
a(t+B)-a(t)=B integral_0^1 a'(t+lambda B)d lambda and using (8).
Adding g(T)Pi_pointer(G_w^*-G_w) can therefore be absorbed into the
constraint multipliers in the first-order action. This is classical
constraint equivalence within the frozen model. Quantizing (9) is an
explicit representative/ordering choice, not a proof that an independently
ordered original nonlinear probe is the same quantum operator.

## 2. A direct forward-coordinate Gaussian lift is impossible

Let mu_0 be the mean-zero version of MPQ's Fourier Gaussian: each real
n>=1 sine/cosine coefficient of sigma has variance (1+n^2)^(-4).
It is a probability measure on E_0=H^3_0(S^1), with Cameron--Martin
space H^4_0. A tempting gauge measure describes a forward embedding by

    F(x)=alpha+integral_0^x exp(sigma(t))dt / integral_0^1 exp(sigma),

using sigma distributed by mu_0. The shape sigma is the spatial mean-zero
part of log F'. Right composition F -> F composed with g acts on it by

    A_g sigma=P_0[sigma composed with g+log g'],           (10)

where g is any smooth increasing degree-one circle lift and P_0 removes
the spatial mean. This is an invertible affine map of H^3_0.

**IGQ-T2 (general forward Gaussian obstruction).** If g is not a rigid
rotation, (A_g)_*mu_0 and mu_0 are mutually singular. This remains true
after any strictly positive density change in the same measure class.
Consequently there is no unitary implementer of (10) on this configuration
multiplication representation, even after tensoring an arbitrary spectator
Hilbert space. In particular no self-adjoint spatial constraint generator
can implement its non-rigid finite flow under this contract. This does
not forbid formal commutators on a smaller domain, other measure classes,
inverse-coordinate measures, or MPQ's gauge-invariant physical fields.

Proof by a measurable path statistic. The distributional derivative

    B_sigma=(2 pi)^(-4) partial_x^3 sigma

has a continuous version with quadratic variation dx. To see this
without a covariance-symbol extrapolation, compare its real Fourier
coefficients with those of a mean-zero periodic Brownian bridge. After
an orthogonal sine/cosine rotation the latter coefficients are independent
Gaussians of amplitude 1/(2 pi n), whereas those of B_sigma have ratio

    r_n=n^4/(1+n^2)^2,
    1-r_n=(2n^2+1)/(1+n^2)^2=O(n^-2).                   (11)

Couple them with the same standard Gaussians. The difference has an
almost surely absolutely convergent first-derivative Fourier series,
because its expected absolute coefficient sum is O(sum n^-2). Thus
B_sigma is a periodic Brownian bridge plus a C^1 random function. A
centered periodic Brownian bridge is an ordinary bridge minus its spatial
mean; subtraction does not change quadratic variation. Brownian squared
increments on a deterministic mesh have mean its total length and
variance bounded by twice the length times the maximum mesh size.
Chebyshev and Borel--Cantelli give the almost-sure dyadic limit; the
same argument applies to the smooth images of dyadic meshes used below.
The bridge's linear endpoint correction and C^1 terms do not affect it.
This also gives sigma a C^3 version, consistent with its H^3 support.

For sigma_g=A_g sigma the third derivative chain rule gives

    B_(sigma_g)=(g')^3 B_sigma composed with g + V,

where V is C^1: it contains sigma'' and sigma' and smooth derivatives
of g and log g'. A deterministic smooth multiplier squares in quadratic
variation, and an increasing change of parameter contributes g'. Hence

    d[B_(sigma_g)]_x=(g'(x))^7 dx.                       (12)

In particular the total dyadic variation is 1 for mu_0 almost every
path and integral_0^1(g')^7 dx for (A_g)_*mu_0 almost every path.
Since integral g'=1, strict convexity makes the latter number >1 unless
g'=1 everywhere. These disjoint full-measure measurable sets prove
singularity. For example g(x)=x+sin(2 pi x)/(4 pi) gives

    integral(g')^7=4587/1024,                            (13)

whereas its mean derivative is still 1. Rotations preserve mu_0 and
are not obstructed by this theorem.

Configuration spectral projections of null sets are zero. A unitary
normalizing that multiplication algebra must preserve its null-set class,
which (12) forbids. Changing phases, a half-density correction or an
equivalent positive weight cannot turn mutually singular measures into
equivalent ones. The non-rigid flow of any nonconstant smooth spatial
smearing encounters this obstruction for sufficiently small nonzero time.

All finite Fourier marginals of these two Gaussian measures are mutually
absolutely continuous, but their Hellinger affinities tend to zero as the
number of modes tends to infinity. For completeness, let eta=(mu+nu)/2.
The conditional densities p_N,q_N on the increasing Fourier sigma-fields
converge in L^1(eta) to p,q. The square roots converge in L^2, so their
inner products tend to integral sqrt(pq)deta=0. Thus positive finite-mode
densities alone cannot establish a continuum gauge lift.

## 3. Inverse embeddings give a quasi-invariant gauge measure

We now use the same covariance for log derivatives of the **inverse**
embeddings, not the forward ones. Write

    chi_epsilon(x)=alpha_epsilon+I_(sigma_epsilon)(x),
    I_sigma(x)=integral_0^x k_sigma(t)dt,
    k_sigma=exp(sigma)/integral_0^1 exp(sigma),
    sigma_epsilon in H^3_0.                              (14)

The common integer identification of chi_+,chi_- is retained. Coordinates
on this space are beta=alpha_+ mod 1, delta=alpha_--alpha_+ in R,
and sigma_+,sigma_-. Select the probability measure

    dnu=d beta rho(delta)d delta dmu_0(sigma_+)dmu_0(sigma_-),
    rho(delta)=(2 pi)^(-1/2)exp(-delta^2/2).              (15)

The finite-dimensional Gaussian is a gauge representation choice; it is
not a physical relative-clock probability. The measure is on Sobolev
embeddings with positive continuous derivative (in fact H^4), a specified
configuration completion of the smooth source chart.

Let G=(Diff_tilde_+^infinity(S^1) x Diff_tilde_+^infinity(S^1))/Z_diag.
It acts by chi_epsilon -> g_epsilon composed with chi_epsilon. The common
integer subgroup acts identically on the configuration quotient. For one
factor the explicit shape transformation is

    sigma -> sigma+u_g(alpha,sigma),
    u_g=P_0 log g'(alpha+I_sigma).                       (16)

**IGQ-T3 (inverse-coordinate quasi-invariance).** Every fixed element
of G acts nonsingularly on (15): its pushforward is mutually absolutely
continuous with nu. This is a continuum result obtained from Ramer's
Gaussian transformation theorem, with its hypotheses verified below.
It is not an application of ordinary translation quasi-invariance to a
random shift without checking its derivative.

Proof. On local angular charts the ambient space is
R^2 x H^3_0 x H^3_0, with Cameron--Martin space
R^2 x H^4_0 x H^4_0. The angular Haar factor is locally equivalent to a
nondegenerate finite Gaussian. The transformation is a homeomorphism with
inverse supplied by g_+^{-1},g_-^{-1}; it is smooth in these Sobolev
charts. The finite-coordinate shifts are smooth. Each u_g maps H^3_0
continuously into H^4_0, because k_sigma is H^3, I_sigma is H^4, and
composition with the fixed smooth function log g' preserves H^4.

We verify the nontrivial Hilbert--Schmidt condition explicitly. For a
Cameron--Martin direction h,

    D I_sigma[h](x)=integral_0^x k_sigma(t)
                    [h(t)-integral k_sigma h]dt,
    D_sigma u_g[h]=P_0[(g''/g')(chi)D I_sigma[h]].        (17)

The first function is periodic with zero endpoint difference. For the
H^4-normalized Fourier vectors h_n=e_n/(1+n^2)^2, Leibniz's rule through
three derivatives of k_sigma h_n gives

    ||D I_sigma[h_n]||_H4
        <=C_sigma (1+n)^3/(1+n^2)^2 <=8 C_sigma/n.       (18)

The normalization subtraction in (17) adds a rank-one term, with bound
O((1+n^2)^-2). Multiplication by (g''/g')(chi), an H^4 function, is
bounded on H^4. Summing the squared bound (18) proves that D_sigma u_g
is Hilbert--Schmidt. Finite alpha/delta derivatives and cross terms are
finite-rank or finite-dimensional. All estimates are locally uniform in
the H^3 coordinates; dominated summation gives continuity into the
Hilbert--Schmidt operators. Finally the derivatives of the inverse map
have the same properties. The chain rule restricted to the Cameron--Martin
space makes I+D u invertible there with bounded inverse.

These are Ramer's local hypotheses: a homeomorphism onto an open set,
a continuous Cameron--Martin-valued displacement, a continuous
Hilbert--Schmidt Cameron--Martin derivative, and invertible derivative
of the full map. They imply mutual absolute continuity on each chart.
A countable cover and the inverse transformation give the global claim.
The applicable theorem and primary research source are identified in
SOURCE_MAP.md; no conclusion from its unrelated PDE model is imported.

The repair has a concrete reason: (10) composes the rough random profile
with g and changes its path variation. Equation (16) instead adds an
H^4 function to an H^3 profile; its third derivative has finite variation,
so the same path statistic is retained. This observation alone is not the
proof of quasi-invariance; the Hilbert--Schmidt and inverse checks are
essential. Also, this new inverse-coordinate measure is not claimed
equivalent to the failed forward-coordinate Gaussian measure.

## 4. Genuine finite gauge unitaries and one-parameter constraint domains

Let H_g=L^2(nu), and let R_g=d(g_*nu)/dnu be the positive Radon--Nikodym
derivative. Set

    U_g psi(chi)=R_g(chi)^(1/2) psi(g^{-1} chi).          (19)

**IGQ-T4 (positive finite gauge representation).** Equation (19) defines
unitaries with U_g U_h=U_(gh) exactly, U_e=I, U_g^*=U_(g^{-1}). There
is no projective phase. The representation respects the common integer
quotient. Every smooth one-parameter subgroup g_f(t) has a strongly
continuous unitary group and self-adjoint generator Q(f), with

    U_(g_f(t))=exp[-it Q(f)],
    Dom Q(f)={psi:lim_(t->0) i[U_(g_f(t))psi-psi]/t
                              exists in H_g}.           (20)

For the source chiral flows the corresponding operators are
T_epsilon[f]=-epsilon Q_epsilon(f). Opposite factors strongly commute.
For each common real smearing f, H[f]=T_+[f]+T_-[f] and
D[f]=T_+[f]-T_-[f] have their self-adjoint joint-spectral realizations.
Their domains are the maximal domains of the respective sums/differences
in the joint spectral measure. They quantize the source moment maps (6)
in the declared inverse-embedding representation.

Proof. Change of variables gives norm preservation. The density identity
R_(gh)(chi)=R_g(chi)R_h(g^{-1}chi) gives the exact group law. The left
actions of the two factors commute, hence so do their unitary groups
and their spectral measures. The common integer action is the identity,
with density one; no large-gauge character is inserted.

For clarity, strong continuity of a flow is not assumed just from the
word 'unitary'. The action is jointly Borel on a standard Borel space.
A jointly measurable density version can be obtained by ratios of
pushforward masses on increasing finite generating partitions and the
martingale limit. Therefore t -> U_(g_f(t)) is a measurable unitary
representation of R on the separable H_g. Vectors of the form
integral a(t)U_(g_f(t))v dt, with a smooth of compact support, are norm
continuous under that flow by L^1 continuity of translation of a. Their
span is dense: an orthogonal vector would be orthogonal to U_t v for
almost every t and a countable dense family of v, hence would be zero.
Unitarity extends continuity to all of H_g. Stone's theorem proves (20).

This is a choice of quantum moment-map realization after an explicit
classical canonical change, not a unitary equivalence to LQA or to a
pre-existing q,p,phi,k,rho field representation. No pointwise inverse-p
operator is obtained merely by rewriting (6).

There is a deliberate domain boundary. A collection of self-adjoint
one-parameter generators is not by itself a dense common domain for
all mixed products and all-smearing Lie commutators. Nor does a genuine
group representation justify differentiating arbitrary vectors twice.
Constructing an invariant smooth-vector/test domain and identifying its
distributional constraint kernel remain next obligations. No all-mode
common-core anomaly theorem is claimed in this packet. The exact finite
group law is the established quantum closure statement here.

## 5. Empty kinematic invariant space and an explicit partial rigging

**IGQ-T5 (normalizable-kernel obstruction, not physical nonexistence).**
The invariant subspace of H_g under G is zero, and so is that of
H_g tensor H_red for a spectator MPQ factor. Already the plus rotation
subgroup has no invariant normalizable vector. Nevertheless its
generalized kernel has an explicit positive averaged completion.

Proof. For g_+(x)=x+t, g_-=id, the coordinates in (15) transform as

    beta -> beta+t mod 1, delta -> delta-t,
    sigma_+,sigma_- unchanged.                          (21)

If psi were an invariant vector, its squared norm density relative to
nu (integrating spectator norms when present) would define an invariant
finite measure. Its delta marginal would be translation invariant on R.
A finite translation-invariant measure on R is zero: equal masses on
disjoint integer translates of any fixed finite interval force that mass
to vanish. Thus psi=0. This argument says nothing about test-dual states.

For a positive explicit comparison set a=beta+delta mod 1, which is
invariant under (21). Let

    K=L^2(S^1_a x H^3_0 x H^3_0,
                            da dmu_0 dmu_0) tensor H_red.

Multiplication by rho(delta)^(1/2) is a unitary
J:H_g tensor H_red -> L^2(R_delta;K). It changes (19) for (21) into
ordinary translation psi(delta)->psi(delta+t). The generator is i partial_delta
on J^{-1}H^1(R;K), or its negative for the oppositely parametrized flow.
On Phi_rot=J^{-1}[S(R_delta) tensor_alg K_0], with K_0 any fixed dense
test subspace of K, define

    R_rot f=integral (Jf)(delta)d delta.

Exactly as in the earlier one-clock averaging argument,

    integral dt <f,U_t h>=<R_rot f,R_rot h>_K.            (22)

The null quotient completes to K. Its generalized histories in the
original Gaussian gauge coordinate are rho(delta)^(-1/2)v, with physical
norm ||v||_K. Their kinematic norm is infinite for nonzero v. This proves
explicitly that the empty kinematic kernel did not forbid a positive
generalized construction.

The rotations in (21) are not a normal subgroup of the full chiral
diffeomorphism group. The other gauge transformations need not descend
to K. Equation (22) is a controlled subgroup rigging, not an assertion
that one may impose all remaining constraints sequentially on K.

## 6. Exact MPQ interface and what is still needed

Use the positive kinematic product H_kin=H_g tensor H_red, with the
source representatives (6),(9). Gauge transformations act by U_g tensor I.
The MPQ observables and pulse act on the second factor. In particular
G_w^* is represented by I tensor J(w); its pointer and clock construction
strongly commutes with all finite gauge unitaries. This is a genuine
source-gauge-compatible kinematic extension of MPQ for the declared
canonical chart and representative choice. It is not yet its physical
derivation from the full source constraints.

**IGQ-T6 (precise reduction criterion).** Suppose a specified gauge test
space Phi_g admits a positive invariant rigging eta_g whose quotient
completion is one-dimensional, with nonzero amplitude map r_g. Then
on Phi_g tensor_alg Phi_red the product rigging has inner product

    eta(f tensor v)[h tensor u]
            =conj(r_g f) r_g h <v,u>_MPQ.                (23)

Its completion is unitarily H_red through f tensor v -> (r_g f)v.
It intertwines all declared MPQ observables and their domains on the
physical factor, and the clock/pointer construction gives exactly MPQ's
H_red tensor L^2(pointer), probabilities and backreaction. If the gauge
physical quotient has additional channels, this product construction
instead retains those channels and is not automatically MPQ alone.

Proof. Equation (23) is the tensor product of the proposed positive
gauge form and the already established MPQ inner product. Taking a test
with r_g f=1 makes its image dense; quotient and completion give the
claimed unitary. The physical observables act only on the v factor, so
their spectral measures and domains are transported by that unitary.
RPM/MPQ clock averaging applies to this actual system space. No existence
of eta_g has been used or proved by this conditional argument.

The decisive missing result is now concrete: a common invariant gauge
test domain for (19),(20), the full generalized invariant kernel, and a
positive rigging with controlled channel count. The subgroup average (22)
does not supply it, and there is no Haar measure on this infinite-
dimensional group being silently inserted. A different ordering of the
original nonlinear source observables would also require an intertwiner;
the constraint-ideal replacement (9) is declared, not hidden.

Closed here: the exact source embedding/momentum map and off-shell
conjugate correction; impossibility of the forward Gaussian lift and
