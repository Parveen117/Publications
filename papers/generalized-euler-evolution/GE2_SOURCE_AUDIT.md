For a general state-dependent clock a=D_Phi g, changing the derivative
also changes its square: D_g^2 f=a^(-2)D_Phi^2 f-a^(-3)(D_Phi a)D_Phi f.
GE2 therefore does not substitute the label "recognition D" into the
existing heat expression. It proves the specific first- and second-order
limits on the native core. Its history ledger is additive on retained
arrows, so it need not be a single-valued strictly increasing state clock
around a closed phase loop.

## Method lineage

Rational unitary steps are a Cayley-transform construction. Squared
increment budgets, symmetric-turn diffusion approximations, contraction
product telescopes and weighted record-variance identities have classical
mathematical lineage. They are not claimed as new general mathematics.
The increment here is the explicit native Recognition–Seam instantiation,
the rational-arrow constants and the clock/domain/scope audit.

For broader repeated-interaction limits, see S. Attal and Y. Pautrat,
[From repeated to continuous quantum interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0311002).
The abstract was checked as lineage context. Its quantum stochastic or
Lindblad results are not imported as proof premises, and GE2 is not an
equivalent reconstruction of that much broader theory. GE1 separately
records Gomilko–Tomilov's Euler approximation lineage; YM52 records the
compact spectral formula's Lauret lineage.

no write credentials. The frozen GE1 workflow also discovers the new
