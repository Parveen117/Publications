Classical primary references checked for attribution:

- NIST DLMF 3.5(v), Gauss quadrature and its polynomial exactness
  through degree 2N-1: <https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.5#v>.
- NIST DLMF 18.2(iv), orthogonal-polynomial recurrence relations,
  and 18.2(x), continued fractions: <https://dlmf.nist.gov/18.2#iv>.

The depth-boundary and response identities are finite forms of this
classical orthogonal-polynomial/positive-quadrature structure. They
are derived explicitly here from the retained Hankel forms and Schur
elimination. The binary deletion rule uses elementary binomial and
conditional-probability identities. The L2 statement is derived
directly using the conditional Bernoulli law of ILC's measure; no
unproved independence of the actual observer sequence is assumed.
No new priority for Gaussian quadrature, Schur complementation,
projection dilation or binomial moment theory is claimed.

The adapter takes trusted source data, with finite positivity guards.
Its algebra alone does not identify arbitrary numeric input with
the completed xi source. The verifier obtains actual inputs only
from the pinned LAA certificate. Independent proof audit covers the
boundary identity, determinant innovation, record consistency, and
strict obstruction to natural cut/wave intertwining.
