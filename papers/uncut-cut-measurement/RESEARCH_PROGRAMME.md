# Research programme after v0.2

## Foundational decision

Keep both candidate starts explicit until a derivation distinguishes them:

- **Ground with admissibility:** uncut ground carries constraints on possible
  cuts and continuations; observations reveal a represented part of that law.
- **Relation generated by distinction:** the first cut creates the first
  meaningful continuation relation; compatibility across further cuts constrains
  admissible laws.

U1–U12 do not choose between them. Neither candidate identifies a labelled finite
set, a quantum state vector or a spacetime point with uncut ground.

## Completed in v0.2: more than one admissible continuation

[U8–U12](FAMILY_CONTINUATION.md) provide the coarsest common closed observer,
exact repair memory, invariance under redundant composite generators, joint-cut
compatibility and a shortest distinguishing experiment. W5 proves that testing
each generator separately can miss a distinction revealed by a mixed word.
W6 shows that a finer initial cut can ask a harder future-prediction question.

Evidence includes 27 regression tests, 3,678 exhaustive cut/two-arrow cases
through three states and 17 explicit four-state fixtures. The foundational
v0.1 checks remain in the same source-bound certificate.

## Next concrete module: which experiments are admissible?

The present family model allows every word of total arrows on one carrier.
Extend it to typed or partial arrows and recover the middle recognition state
before composing. Record which interventions are enabled at a candidate state;
an experiment that is undefined or forbidden cannot be used as a diagnostic
witness. Prove a closure result that respects these restrictions, and distinguish
inapplicability from a negative measurement. This connects directly to the
native recovered-middle-identity requirement in pinned source S1.

Acceptance conditions:

1. The arrow family and target are fixed before assessing closure.
2. Every claimed comparison map is well-defined on whole fibres.
3. A counterexample must show how ignoring admissibility invents a false diagnostic word.
4. Extra memory is operationally identified; a decoder is not an instrument.
5. Exhaustive finite evidence and the general proof have separate statuses.

## Physical measurement adapter

Specify an instrument, its admissible interventions, raw records and the target
that matters. A proposed repair predicts an additional observable before its
value is acquired. Check whether that observable distinguishes the predicted
fibre classes on held-out measurements. Permit a failed prediction to reject
the presentation or the proposed adapter. Preserve raw data and all model
versions. Noise bounds and unmodelled alternatives need explicit treatment;
model completeness cannot be certified by a hash.

## Cut cost and healing

Start from target ambiguity or missing continuation memory. Define allowed
paths and a cost on them before selecting any observed path. Determine whether
the cost is additive, subadditive or otherwise compositional and prove the
relevant law. An observer's minimum memory requirement and a minimum action
principle for nature are different claims; a bridge needs its own theorem.

## Quantum and classical descriptions

Construct the native amplitudes or alternative predictive objects, their
composition, normalization, probability/readout rule and interference. Establish
the operational conditions under which a classical description closes. Test
whether the same cut-memory data explains the transition and predicts new
measurements. Compare to existing repository quantum witnesses only after this
construction; do not assume their quantum formalism as a primitive here.

## Spacetime as a representation

First derive an order or locality structure from declared composition, if it
exists. Then state conditions for a geometric representation and determine
which parts of the native law it preserves. Metric signature, dimension,
physical duration and matter coupling each require definitions and proof.
If a sector admits no spacetime representation, say exactly which native
predictions remain meaningful. Finite graph distance alone does not establish
a physical spacetime metric.

## Curvature, information and the EMK tensor

Choose a specific representation and define both the composition residue and
the target-relative information quantity. Prove an adapter between their
domains before asserting duality. Identify cases with zero observed residue
but hidden target ambiguity, and cases with a faithful observer but nonzero
composition residue. A universal EMK tensor requires these distinctions to be
resolved within its stated hypotheses rather than hidden by a single symbol.

## Publication gates

v0.2 is a public working draft with exact finite evidence. A later paper edition
should add independently checked proofs, a complete empirical or physical
identification where claimed, and a compiled manuscript matching its claim
ledger. A GitHub release/DOI follows the repository's existing release rule;
opening this research programme does not create a DOI or replace an arXiv paper.
