## External analytic inputs

[FH] The Feldman--Hajek Gaussian equivalence criterion, in the explicit
form of Appendix B, Theorem B.1, of David Bolin and Kristin Kirchner,
*Equivalence of measures and asymptotically optimal linear prediction
for Gaussian random fields with fractional-order covariance operators*,
arXiv:2101.07860v2 (17 February 2022):
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.07860.
LMD checks its Cameron--Martin range, mean and Hilbert--Schmidt hypotheses
on H^(-delta); it does not infer equivalence from finite marginals.
The elementary Gaussian Hellinger/density formulas are used locally
near identity to justify strong continuity and differentiation.

[GMC] Nathanael Berestycki, *An elementary approach to Gaussian
multiplicative chaos*, arXiv:1506.09113v2, Theorem 1.1, covariance
estimates in Lemma 3.5 and regularization identification in Section 5:
https://arxiv.org/html/1506.09113v2.
Alexander Shamov, *On Gaussian multiplicative chaos*, arXiv:1407.4418:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.4418.
Here the kernel is log(1/distance) plus a smooth remainder in every
circle chart, the reference is Lebesgue length, and the field amplitude
is sqrt(h). We restrict to h<1, where the second-moment kernel is
integrable. The martingale, moment, nonzero and atomless conclusions
are proved directly; the compatible mollifier identification is the
standard L2 uniqueness argument. No critical or supercritical chaos
result is imported.

Stone's theorem, Cameron--Martin translation, Gaussian moment identities,
martingale convergence, the zero-one law and Borel--Cantelli are
standard analytic inputs. They are not machine-proved by the packet.

The contribution is the source-specific choice and consequences:
the required covariance exponent, an actual local-flow representation,
the correction 1+h/2 forced by the chosen density normalization, a
domain-safe feedback identity, and the explicit T^3 shear diagnostic.
No novelty claim is made for Gaussian equivalence, Wick exponentiation,
Gaussian multiplicative chaos or the general quantum background-charge
mechanism.

## Scope

The old MPQ/RGR quantum observable contract is not replaced. LMD is a
different reduced representation. Its total-charge rotation has the
same spectrum but this does not establish equivalence of the full
configuration algebras. The original pointwise inverse geometry and
all unreduced nonlinear orderings remain open.

The three-dimensional shear theorem tests this scalar Fourier measure
under one actual spatial diffeomorphism. It is not a no-go for tensor
gravity, nor proof of a 3+1 gravitational constraint algebra.
