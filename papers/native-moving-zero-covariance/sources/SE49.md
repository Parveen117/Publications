# SE49: scalar observer innovations, a quantitative response limit, and eta zero on V_24

13 October 2026 (Asia/Kolkata). Author: Monty.

This stage extends the complete-source eta-zero certificate from V_16
to V_24. It also makes SE48's convergence contract quantitative on the
actual integer-rate observation bank. Each new observation has two
explicit scalar sign conditions. Their universal validity remains open;
the finite extension and the convergence implication do not prove RH.

Typed Cut, derived scalars and iota, lawful composition and retained
source readings precede the admitted representation. The source, pairing,
boundary column and accepted four-space are those of SE21/SE39/SE47/SE48.
The coordinate change below does not redefine the source or its sign.
Complex analysis is an explicit adapter applied to the acquired response,
not a new native primitive. Lambda, source shift eta and spectral mu
remain different quantities.

## 1. Theorem 1: two innovations decide whether a new reading is admissible

Keep SE48's actual response and its two acquired forms:

\[
 q(t)=\frac{\mathcal X'(t)}{\mathcal X(t)}
      =\frac{Q(1/2+\sqrt t)}{2\sqrt t},\qquad
 L_{ij}=-q[t_i,t_j],\quad J_{ij}=(tq(t))[t_i,t_j],\quad
 S_N=4J_N.                                                     \tag{49.1}
\]

Here Q=xi'/xi, t_i=(i+3/2)^2 and u_i=q(t_i). Repeated divided
differences mean derivatives. Assume the existing L_N and J_N are
positive definite. At a new positive x distinct from all old nodes,
read the **actual** r=q(x) and d=q'(x), and retain the cross readings

\[
 \ell_i=\frac{u_i-r}{x-t_i},\qquad
 j_i=\frac{xr-t_i u_i}{x-t_i}.
\]

**Theorem.** The two enlarged forms are positive definite exactly when

\[
 \boxed{\alpha=-d-\ell^T L_N^{-1}\ell>0,\qquad
        \beta=r+xd-j^T J_N^{-1}j>0.}                           \tag{49.2}
\]

Equivalently the new derivative lies in the strict measured window

\[
 \boxed{\frac{j^T J_N^{-1}j-r}{x}<d< -\ell^T L_N^{-1}\ell.}    \tag{49.3}
\]

The width is
(r-j^T J_N^(-1)j-x ell^T L_N^(-1)ell)/x. It need not be positive
for arbitrary new value r. Existing positivity does not force a new
actual reading into this window.

**Proof.** The enlarged matrices are

\[
 L_{N+1}=\begin{pmatrix}L_N&\ell\\\ell^T&-d\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 J_{N+1}=\begin{pmatrix}J_N&j\\j^T&r+xd\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Completing the square in each form gives respectively
diag(L_N,alpha) and diag(J_N,beta) by invertible triangular
congruence. This proves both directions and (49.3). QED.

There is also an exact update for the response, rather than just its
matrix sign. For real z>0 set

\[
 \begin{split}
 P&=J_N+zL_N,\quad k=j+z\ell,\quad
 \delta=r+(x-z)d-k^T P^{-1}k,\\
 e&=r-k^T P^{-1}u,\qquad q_N(z)=u^T P^{-1}u.
 \end{split}
\]

Under (49.2), block elimination proves

\[
 \boxed{q_{N+1}(z)-q_N(z)=\frac{e^2}{\delta}\ge0,\qquad
        \delta\ge\beta+z\alpha>0.}                            \tag{49.4}
\]

For the last inequality, minimize the enlarged J form plus z times
the enlarged L form over its old coordinates, keeping the new
coordinate equal to one. The minimum of the sum is at least the sum
of the separate minima, beta+z alpha. Thus the nonnegative response
increment is at most e^2/(beta+z alpha). This is an update of SE48's
spectral response; it is distinct from SE47's five-column H update.

The monotonicity in (49.4) concerns finite responses at a positive real
test point. It neither proves their convergence to the actual source
nor proves that each q_N is below actual q. Sections 2 and 3 address
those questions with their required domains and hypotheses.

For the initial node, use alpha_1=L_11 and beta_1=J_11, without an
empty inverse. Subsequent alpha_n,beta_n are the usual leading LDL
pivots of the two forms. They are coordinate-dependent residual
readings, not invariant spectral gaps; their signs decide extension
because the full congruences and mixed terms have been retained.

## 2. Theorem 2: a finite, unconditional bound against the actual response

Let w_j=j+3/2, w_1=5/2, and suppose just the actual finite L_N,J_N
are positive definite. No sign beyond N is assumed. Write

\[
 B_{N,a}(w)=\prod_{j=1}^{N}
       \left|\frac{w-w_j}{w+w_j-2a}\right|^2
       \quad(\operatorname{Re}w>a),\qquad
 c=w_1q(w_1^2)>0.                                            \tag{49.5}
\]

**Theorem.** On Re w>1, the actual response satisfies

\[
 \boxed{\left|q(w^2)-q_N(w^2)\right|
       \le\frac{|w+5/2|^2}{|w|}\,B_{N,1}(w).}                \tag{49.6}
\]

All arithmetic and analytic tails of actual q are included. In
particular, no zero catalogue, presumed zero-free critical strip or
assumed RH/Stieltjes representation of actual q enters this bound.
The zero-free input here is the Euler-product half-plane Re s>1.
Our bound uses the smaller domain Re s>3/2 for a convenient explicit
constant. The finitely checked hypotheses hold for every N<=24.

**Proof of the majorant.** Put

\[
 F(w)=wq(w^2)=\tfrac12 Q(1/2+w),\qquad
 F_N(w)=wq_N(w^2).
\]

SE48 gives q_N(z)=sum_k a_k/(z+mu_k), a_k,mu_k>0. Therefore

\[
 \frac{w}{w^2+\mu}
 =\frac12\left(\frac1{w-\iota\sqrt\mu}
                 +\frac1{w+\iota\sqrt\mu}\right)
\]

has positive real part on Re w>0, as does F_N. The Cayley function
h_N=(F_N-c)/(F_N+c) maps this half-plane into the unit disk and
vanishes at w_1. The disk Schwarz inequality gives

\[
 |h_N(w)|\le\rho(w):=\left|\frac{w-w_1}{w+w_1}\right|<1,
 \quad
 |F_N(w)|\le c\frac{1+\rho}{1-\rho}
       \le q(w_1^2)\frac{|w+w_1|^2}{\operatorname{Re}w}.       \tag{49.7}
\]

The last bound follows from
|w+w_1|^2-|w-w_1|^2=4w_1 Re w and
|w-w_1|<=|w+w_1|. The complete reading gives
0<q(w_1^2)<1/2 (approximately 0.02287813905).

For actual F, let s=1/2+w and Re w>1. The Euler product, differentiated
in its absolutely convergent half-plane, gives

\[
 \left|\frac{\zeta'}\zeta(s)\right|
 \le\sum_{n\ge2}\frac{\log n}{n^{3/2}}<5.                    \tag{49.8}
\]

For completeness f(x)=log(x)/x^(3/2) decreases on [2,infinity);
the sum is at most f(2)+integral_2^infinity f(x)dx
=(5 log 2/2+4)/sqrt 2 < 13/(2 sqrt 2) <5.
The standard partial-fraction identity
psi(v)=-gamma-1/v+sum_(k>=1) v/[k(k+v)] implies, for Re v>3/4,

\[
 |\psi(v)|<\tfrac73+2|v|,
\]

using 0<gamma<1 and sum k^(-2)<2. With v=s/2, 0<log pi<2 and the
exact completed response formula, this gives

\[
 |Q(s)|<10+|s|/2,\qquad |F(w)|<41/8+|w|/4.
\]

Since |w+w_1|>=7/2 and |w+w_1|>=|w|,

\[
 \left|\frac{F(w)}{(w+w_1)^2}\right|
 <\frac{41/8}{49/4}+\frac1{14}=\frac{24}{49}<\frac12.
\]

Equation (49.7) similarly bounds F_N/(w+w_1)^2 by less than 1/2
on this half-plane. Hence g_N=(F-F_N)/(w+w_1)^2 is holomorphic
and bounded in absolute value by one there.

**Proof of the interpolation bound.** Actual q and q_N have the same
value and derivative at every t_j. Thus g_N has a zero of order at
least two at every w_j. A bounded analytic function in Re w>a
with a zero at a real b>a can be divided by (w-b)/(w+b-2a)
without increasing its supremum norm. To see this, map the half-plane
to the disk with b at its centre, apply the Schwarz inequality and
remove the zero analytically. Repeat for every zero, including its
multiplicity. Consequently |g_N(w)|<=B_(N,1)(w).
Multiplication by |w+w_1|^2/|w| proves (49.6). QED.

This is a finite bound for each certified N. Claiming that the same
sequence of certified objects exists at every N would still require
the as-yet unproved infinite sequence of signs (49.2).

## 3. Theorem 3: quantitative convergence on the actual integer bank

For two nested finite positive pencils of orders M>N, the same
Schwarz-factor argument applied to (h_M-h_N)/2 on Re w>0 proves

\[
 \boxed{|q_M(w^2)-q_N(w^2)|
 \le \frac{4c}{|w|(1-\rho(w))^2} B_{N,0}(w).}                 \tag{49.9}
\]

Indeed the bounded function has double zeros at all old w_j. The
inverse Cayley formula F=c(1+h)/(1-h) and (49.7) supply the displayed
denominator. This finite-pair inequality does not presume all orders.

**Theorem.** On the fixed actual bank t_j=(j+3/2)^2, the following
are equivalent:

1. RH.
2. L_N and J_N are positive definite for every N.
3. The initial two signs and both innovations (49.2) are positive at
   every successive actual observation.

Under these equivalent hypotheses the finite responses converge
locally uniformly to actual q on C minus the nonpositive real axis.
Their full tail obeys

\[
 \boxed{|q(w^2)-q_N(w^2)|
 \le\frac{4c}{|w|(1-\rho(w))^2}B_{N,0}(w)
 \quad(\operatorname{Re}w>0).}                              \tag{49.10}
\]

This is a proved implication and criterion; the universal hypotheses
are not established by the execution below.

**Proof.** The equivalence of (2) and (3) is Theorem 1 by induction.
Under RH the genus-zero response in SE39 is
sum_gamma m_gamma/(t+gamma^2). The respective Cauchy Gram weights
for L and J are m_gamma and m_gamma gamma^2. A nonzero finite
rational combination cannot vanish at infinitely many distinct
gamma^2, so both forms are strictly positive. This proves (1)=>(2).

For (2)=>(1), first note the quantitative property of the actual
integer bank. On any fixed compact K in Re w>0, with
a_K=min_(w in K) Re w>0,

\[
 B_{N,0}(w)\le C_K N^{-4a_K}\longrightarrow0
 \quad\hbox{uniformly for }w\in K.                           \tag{49.11}
\]

To verify it, discard a fixed finite number of factors. For the
remaining ones the logarithm is
-4 Re(w)/w_j+O_K(w_j^(-3)); the latter errors are summable and
sum_(j<=N) 1/w_j=log N+O(1). Points that coincide with a probe
have an exactly zero product and already satisfy the bound.
The same calculation gives decay N^(-4(Re w-1)) for B_(N,1)
on compact subsets of Re w>1.

Thus (49.9) makes the sequence locally uniformly Cauchy on Re w>0.
Its limit is holomorphic there. On Re w>1, the independently proved
actual-source bound (49.6) identifies that limit with actual q(w^2).
The identity theorem for the meromorphic actual response extends the
identification throughout Re w>0. A zero of xi with Re s>1/2
would give F=Q/2 a nonremovable pole there, contradicting the
holomorphic limit. Functional-equation reflection excludes left-side
zeros as well. Therefore RH holds. Taking M to infinity in (49.9)
proves (49.10). QED.

Unlike SE48's analytic proof, this argument does not change to an
interior-accumulating observation bank. It supplies an explicit tail
on the very integer-rate bank used in the arithmetic replay. It
does not import a Stieltjes measure for actual q before proving its
existence. SE21's density route remains valid independently.

The node density matters. Mere unboundedness is insufficient: for
w=1/8 and w_j=2^j, each squared factor is at least 1-2^(-j-1),
so every finite product is greater than 1/2, and the infinite product
is at least 1/2. On the actual diagonal boundary Re w=0, every
factor has modulus one. Neither (49.11) nor a positive-distance
error bound is uniform as the observation approaches that boundary.
This theorem excludes off-line poles under its universal premise;
it does not give a finite count or uniform localization of all
critical-line zeros.

## 4. Theorem 4: certify innovations after a lawful basis change

The first direct interval evaluation was inconclusive at innovation
17 for L and at innovation 18 for J. Those boxes contained zero;
they were not evidence of a negative reading. Dependency in repeated
interval elimination had overwhelmed the small positive residual.

Let A be either real symmetric actual form and let T be any exact
invertible upper-triangular matrix. Set B=T^T A T. Suppose outward
arithmetic encloses B and proves its positivity, for example by
strict positive LDL pivots. Then A is positive definite. If p_i(B)
are its leading LDL pivots, the actual untransformed innovations are

\[
 \boxed{p_i(A)=p_i(B)/T_{ii}^2.}                              \tag{49.12}
\]

**Proof.** Congruence preserves the sign. Since T is upper triangular,
the first i transformed columns use only the first i source states.
For each leading block det B_i=(prod_(k<=i) T_kk^2) det A_i.
Taking adjacent determinant ratios proves (49.12). QED.

The replay uses a 120-digit midpoint LDL factorization only to propose
T. Its rounded entries become exact rational numbers. The unchanged
90-digit outward arithmetic then recomputes T^T A T from the complete
source boxes. Both outward LDL and strictly positive Gershgorin row
margins check the transformed forms. The proposal is never accepted
as a sign certificate. This is a computational coordinate repair,
not a positive correction to A or a discarded residue.

## 5. What the development leaves to prove

The exact remaining arithmetic statement in this route is

\[
 \alpha_n>0\quad\hbox{and}\quad\beta_n>0\qquad
 \text{for every actual integer-rate observation }n.
\]

SE49 provides the update, a stable finite certification procedure and
a quantitative limit if that statement holds. It does not show that
the first 24 positive pairs force pair 25 or the infinite sequence.
SE48's symmetric off-line masking control still rules out that
finite-to-global inference.

The global inherited bound remains

\[
 S_b+\frac{1079}{2000}I_X\succeq\frac1{492000}I_X.
\]

Only the proved eta-zero restriction has grown from V_16 to V_24.
The full endpoint remains noncoercive as established in SE48. No
bounded global inverse is postulated, no term is removed by naming
the seam zero, and no universal source sign is built into the Cut.
