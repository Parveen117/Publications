# SE29 Curvature blindness and the source diagonal

12 October 2026. Owner: Monty.

This tests the missing implication in SE28: can flatness of an attached
TVSP connection prove the actual unshifted source inequality? For the
specific canonical effect connection already present in thermodynamic 08,
we prove an obstruction. Its curvature loses the distinction between
reciprocal observer gains. On the actual SE5 source its curvature norm is
exactly 1/2, including at the essential threshold, independently of whether
the source is nonnegative. A retained diagonal recovers the missing
comparison. Its required sign is not proved here. RH and global eta zero
remain open; the inherited global anchor is still 1079/2000.

The minimum exact observer O_* has ||O_*||^2=||T||. SE5 proves that

\[
 S_b=N^{1/2}(I-T)N^{1/2}.
 \tag{SE29.2}
\]

We attach the existing canonical effect construction to the actual source

\[

This is a declared source adapter. It is not asserted to be the unique

## 2. Theorem 1: Exact curvature and diagonal recovery

On X direct-sum X put

\[
 P=\begin{pmatrix}\mathsf A&H\\H&I-\mathsf A\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 \Gamma=\begin{pmatrix}I&0\\0&-I\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then P=P^dagger=P^2. For the same constant connection coefficients as
thermodynamic 08, A_r=iota Gamma/2 and A_tau=iota P, its self-adjoint
information curvature and its square are

\[
 F=\frac1{2\iota}[\Gamma,P]
   =\begin{pmatrix}0&-\iota H\\\iota H&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 F^2=\begin{pmatrix}K&0\\0&K\end{pmatrix},\quad
 K=T(I+T)^{-2}.
 \tag{SE29.4}
\]

The accepted curvature amplitude is iota H. Its Gram K obeys

\[
 \boxed{\Delta^2+4K=I.}
 \tag{SE29.5}
\]

If J_+x=(x,0), the additional diagonal reading is

\[
 \boxed{\Delta=J_+^\dagger(I-2P)J_+,\qquad
 T=(I-\Delta)(I+\Delta)^{-1}.}
 \tag{SE29.6}
\]

Let Q=N^{-1/2}(I+T)^{-1/2}. It is boundedly invertible and

\[
 \boxed{Q^\dagger S_bQ=\Delta,\qquad
 S_b\succeq0\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \Delta\succeq0
 \ \Longleftrightarrow\ T\preceq I.}
 \tag{SE29.7}
\]

**Proof.** Functional calculus makes A, H and Delta commuting bounded
self-adjoint functions of T. The identities A^2+H^2=A and
AH+H(I-A)=H give P^2=P. Direct block multiplication gives (SE29.4).
The scalar numerator identity (1-t)^2+4t=(1+t)^2 proves (SE29.5).
The first compression in (SE29.6) is I-2A; I+Delta=2(I+T)^(-1)
is boundedly invertible, proving the inverse formula. In (SE29.7),
the N factors cancel in their displayed order; the remaining functions
of T commute. Invertible congruence preserves positivity. No commutation
of N with T has been used. QED.

A positive source shift transforms as
Q^dagger(S_b+eta I)Q=Delta+eta Q^dagger Q. It is not generally
Delta+eta I. Thus (SE29.7) does not reduce the inherited eta by changing
the comparison metric. Nonnegativity is also weaker than Delta=0:
the latter would require T=I and the entire source S_b=0.

## 3. Theorem 2: Curvature is blind to reciprocal gain

If T is positive and boundedly invertible, replacing T by T^(-1)
preserves H, K and the whole F in (SE29.4), while replacing Delta by
-Delta. It also exchanges A and I-A. Consequently neither the full F
of this construction nor its Gram determines which side of T=I the
source occupies. The complete pair (Gamma,P) does retain that information.

**Proof.** Substitution in (SE29.3) gives
T^(-1/2)(I+T^(-1))^(-1)=T^(1/2)(I+T)^(-1), and similarly for K.
The formulas for A and Delta follow by multiplying numerator and
denominator by T. QED.

An exact scalar example is:

| Squared gain t | Curvature amplitude H | Curvature Gram K | Diagonal Delta | Source 1-t |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9/16 | 12/25 | 144/625 | 7/25 | 7/16 |
| 16/9 | 12/25 | 144/625 | -7/25 | -7/9 |

These are supplied countermodels to an implication from curvature alone;
they are not negative vectors for the actual RH source.

There are also countermodels satisfying the relevant infinite-dimensional
threshold hypotheses, not merely finite matrices. On ell^2(N), n>=1, put

\[
 T_c e_n=\left(\frac n{n+1}\right)^2e_n,\qquad
 T_e e_n=\left(\frac{n+1}n\right)^2e_n.
 \tag{SE29.8}
\]

Take N=I, A_*=I, L_*=sqrt(T), so recovery is exact and the mismatch
floor is one in both models. Both have 1 in their essential spectrum,
compact (T-I)_+, and source decomposition S=0-V with signed compact
V=T-I. Nevertheless S_c>=0 and every basis vector has strictly negative
S_e-form. Their accepted amplitude and full curvature agree exactly:

\[
 H_ne_n=\frac{n(n+1)}{2n^2+2n+1}e_n,\qquad
 \Delta_{c,n}=-\Delta_{e,n}
   =\frac{2n+1}{2n^2+2n+1}.
 \tag{SE29.9}
\]

The limits T_c,T_e->1 on basis modes prove the essential-threshold
statements directly, and the differences from I are compact diagonal
operators. The source signs follow from (SE29.2). Thus the qualitative
SE5 operator hypotheses together with the complete curvature signal
still do not force source positivity. Arithmetic structure would have
to supply an additional argument; these models do not contain it.

## 4. Theorem 3: Unavoidable curvature on the actual source

For the actual SE5 source attached by (SE29.3),

\[
 \boxed{0\preceq K\preceq\tfrac14I,\quad
 \|K\|=\|K\|_{\rm ess}=\tfrac14,\qquad
 \|F\|=\|F\|_{\rm ess}=\tfrac12.}
 \tag{SE29.10}
\]

In fact 0 is in the essential spectrum of Delta, 1/4 in that of K,
and both 1/2 and -1/2 in that of F. These conclusions do not assume
S_b>=0. Curvature is therefore not zero at the actual critical source
threshold in this normalization, even if the missing positivity were true.

**Proof.** The scalar inequality 4t<=(1+t)^2 for t>=0 gives the
upper bound for K and hence F. Use the SE5 unit weakly null sequence
z_j with (T-I)z_j->0. Since
Delta z_j=-(I+T)^(-1)(T-I)z_j, it tends to zero. By (SE29.5),
(K-1/4)z_j=-Delta^2 z_j/4->0. Also H+1/2 is invertible, and
(H-1/2)z_j=(H+1/2)^(-1)(K-1/4)z_j->0. The doubled unit weakly
null vectors (z_j,+iota z_j)/sqrt(2) and (z_j,-iota z_j)/sqrt(2)
are approximate eigenvectors for F at +1/2 and -1/2 respectively.
They prove the claimed essential spectral inclusions. A compact
operator takes each such weakly null sequence to zero, so subtracting
any compact operator cannot lower the norm below these thresholds.
The upper bounds already proved yield the equalities. QED.

For fixed connection coordinates and the normalization of thermodynamic
08, unitary gauge changes conjugate F and preserve these norms. They
cannot make it flat. A change of connection or parameter scale is a
different operation; a singular rescaling that shrinks a displayed
component does not prove (SE29.7). This theorem concerns this explicit
source attachment, not every conceivable TVSP connection. In particular
it does not contradict SE28's scalar-flat identification Z_0=zeta.

## 5. Theorem 4: Recovering the missing sector and the remaining proof

Let E_-=1_(-infinity,0)(Delta), equivalently 1_(1,infinity)(T).
The diagonal's negative part is compact:

\[
 (-\Delta)_+=(T-I)_+(I+T)^{-1}.
 \tag{SE29.11}
\]

Its strict negative spectral subspaces away from zero are finite
dimensional, but E_- itself may have infinite rank. The pair (K,E_-)
recovers the source comparison through

\[
 \boxed{\Delta=(I-2E_-)\sqrt{I-4K}.}
 \tag{SE29.12}
\]

**Proof.** In scalar spectral calculus Delta is negative exactly when
t>1, and its negative part is (t-1)_+/(1+t). This proves (SE29.11)
and compactness using SE5. Equation (SE29.5) says sqrt(I-4K)=|Delta|.
Changing the sign precisely on E_- recovers Delta, including its zero
subspace. QED.

The absent information is a spectral sector, not a choice of names for
positive and negative coordinates. Faithful changes of frame transport
this comparison and preserve its zero/nonzero negative-sector status.
One can retain it by measuring the full projector diagonal (SE29.6).
Taking the positive square root in (SE29.12) without E_- already assumes
the inequality being sought; the expanding countermodel detects that error.

The exact remaining source obligation is still

\[
 E_-=0,\quad\text{equivalently}\quad
 \|L_*f\|^2\le\|A_*f\|^2\quad\hbox{for every }f\in X.
 \tag{SE29.13}
\]

Writing this obligation as a zero invariant does not prove its value.
The curvature norms in (SE29.10) cannot decide it: both infinite models
have those same norms. The existing positive-eta source certificates,
finite eta-zero Gram certificate, and SE28 zeta specialization are
retained. None proves (SE29.13). No cofinal zero-shift estimate, RH
completion, source-negative RH vector, or general impossibility of
