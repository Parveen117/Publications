## Attribution

The ideal classification in Z, matrix-unit module argument, Chinese
remainder theorem, p-adic inverse limit, and finite permutation
representations are standard algebra. We give elementary proofs of the
specific statements needed here; we do not claim these general tools
as new discoveries. For standard ideals, quotient rings and Chinese
remaindering see J. S. Milne, *A Primer of Commutative Algebra*, v4.03
(23 March 2020), section 2:
https://www.jmilne.org/math/xnotes/CA.pdf .

NM1 already attributes the modular generator theorem to J. S. Milne's
*Modular Functions and Modular Forms*, while supplying its own Euclidean
proof. That result is reused, not counted as a new POS theorem.

The finite-register dilation reuses CHD and standard coherent measurement
bookkeeping. The lattice-preserving holonomy obstruction uses elementary
discreteness together with the already derived TET small-loop expansion.
The arbitrary-energy constructions are explicit finite spectral
countermodels, not a proposed physical Hamiltonian. For the distinction
between an excitation-energy threshold and the full physical field-theory
target, see A. Jaffe and E. Witten, *Quantum Yang--Mills Theory*:
https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf .

The source-bound result is the seam-projector realization of the prime
observer classification, its carried hierarchy and coherent embeddings,
and its compatibility boundary with the actual TVSP transport. General
algebraic facts and no-go statements do not certify the user's broader
prime-to-physical-mass hypothesis.
