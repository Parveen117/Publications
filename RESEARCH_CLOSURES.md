## What changed in this pass

HQS starts from REC's actual constraint C=L pi k at V=0, not a newly
invented finite toy Hamiltonian. Log-length coordinates give C=p k.
A self-adjoint joint-spectral product and regular-clock group average
produce the positive physical space L^2(dp/|p|). An explicit unitary map
provides self-adjoint length and dilation observables, with length positive and
specified domains. Ordinary half-line momentum fails its extension test.
CHD observer verification is realized on normalizable physical packet spans.

This closes a selected homogeneous quantum sector, adding a physical inner
product and domain analysis absent from REC. It does not turn the existing
finite verification paper into a complete physical QG theory. The manuscript
and its pinned certificate remain unchanged.

## Evidence convention

Each closure keeps its assumptions, general written proof, finite executable
witnesses, negative controls and unresolved physical identifications together.
Standard theorems are credited. A passed numerical or algebraic replay
does not upgrade a model choice into a derived law, or a finite fixture into
a continuum proof-assistant certificate.
