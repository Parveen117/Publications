## What changed in this pass

chiral current factorization. The conventional oscillator vacuum gives c=2
per chirality. Its null derivative improvement cannot cancel the anomaly;
equal chiral charges leave a mixed normal/spatial central defect. The
unchanged strong all-mode constraints have no nonzero common kernel.

A source-preserving alternative reverses the negative-sign oscillator
vacuum. The contributions +1 and -1 cancel, and the inherited improvement
remains null. This gives a positive kinematic Fock representation with exact
local constraint closure on a common dense algebraic domain. The homogeneous
zero-mode equation remains PK=0. The known vacuum method is attributed and
rederived here; this is not a claim of a new Virasoro mechanism.

A separate linear-counterterm family is classified exactly. It changes the
homogeneous equation to PK-pi*hbar/3=0 and is not adopted. HQS's physical
sector, all predecessor certificates and the completed manuscript stay frozen.
