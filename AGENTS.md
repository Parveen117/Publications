# Publication research continuity

## Cross-repository construction rule

Owner instruction, 10 October 2026: this is the construction policy for every
framework repository, not merely a certification or testing convention.

Start new constructions on the declared full lambda/compass carrier. Construct
or consistently lift cuts, relations, numbers, iota, dimension, metric,
quantities, constants and evolution there; then derive the zero-reading and its
established interpretation. Retain source, complementary channels, ordered
history, tensor type and pairing throughout the construction.

For each operation, specify the domain and the appropriate specialization maps
and establish, on that domain, the compatibility requirement

`pi_0(Construct_lambda(inputs)) = Construct_0(pi_0(inputs))`.

Here the maps on inputs and outputs have their respective types. Recover the
established zero/classical case where that identification is justified. A
zero-reading need not erase history or make every notion of curvature vanish.

Declare the primitive carrier and its assumptions explicitly; do not identify
it automatically with a scalar parameter, derivative order, clock time,
coupling or renormalization scale. Construct time/evolution from ordered
composition and retained history, with an explicit clock adapter when needed;
tower depth alone is not elapsed time. Give constants a selection law and
dimensional calibration where applicable.

Certification and tests verify this construction and its claimed recovery.
Checking a supplied classical model alone does not derive the full carrier.
Record unconstructed parts as open obligations. This owner-selected research
policy is not a theorem that a universal lambda-space construction is complete.
Extend existing results through explicit lifts/adapters and new stages; keep
frozen theorem packets, evidence and source snapshots intact. Preserve existing
verification requirements and distinguish proof, finite checks and physical
identification. Carry this rule into future framework repositories as they are
opened for work.

Owner verification preference, 10 October after the policy update: prioritize
the full-family construction and its zero-specialization compatibility. Use
written proofs and only direct checks needed for the changed claim. Do not add
generic test suites, rerun untouched predecessor suites or regenerate frozen
certificates merely to accompany a new stage. Report what was actually checked.

The researcher chooses and justifies its admitted operators, domains,
composition, pairing and zero-reading, and may revise constructions where
needed. Treat intuitive proposals as research directions; preserve valid
earlier proofs and state changed hypotheses explicitly. A scalar interface
coordinate in an application does not identify the whole primitive carrier.

Read the root README, the target paper's index/README and its exact source and
claim boundaries before mathematical work. Use `MATHEMATICAL_FOUNDATION.md`
for common mathematical development. Follow the canonical RKF operator-engine
route documented in this repository; a publication is not a second engine home.
Keep theorems, numerical evidence, physical calibration and open Yang–Mills
continuum obligations distinct. Instruction updates do not revise published
proofs or promote earlier claims.
